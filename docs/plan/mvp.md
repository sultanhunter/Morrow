## What Morrow is

Morrow is a shop's AI team, working alongside its people. A merchant installs Morrow from Shopify and picks agents from Morrow's catalogue. Each agent has a standing goal, such as "every active product has complete SEO" or "no top seller drops under 14 days of stock", and works toward it on its own: on schedules and routines, when the store changes, when a watched number moves, and in a daily look for useful work. It keeps a notebook of what it learned and plans next.

People and agents share one workspace: projects with their goals, a task board, a thread on every task, one Inbox, a shop knowledge base and an activity log. Under [ADR-0033](../decisions/adr/ADR-0033-work-model-v2.md) (accepted 2026-10-24), every goal lives in one project with a measure computed in code, and a project either has an end date or is Everyday work, one area per added agent, with Quick tasks for one-off jobs. The Coordinator ("Morrow") is the main agent and keeps the conversation (ADR-0037). It answers from knowledge, asks clarifying questions and orchestrates its teammates in any way the work needs: it calls them as tools for structured results, in sequence or in parallel and the same teammate more than once, and, outside an Ask, hands work to the teammate that must own the conversation. When no teammate covers the work, it drafts a new one as an agent card from first-party skills and the fixed tool catalogue, which a person approves; the new teammate stays on the team until a person removes it. Teammates never call, hand off to or create agents. Under ADR-0040 and ADR-0041 (accepted 2026-10-26), each person's conversation with Morrow works like messaging a team: a message is seen within seconds; Morrow answers, asks one question, or says what it understood and starts the work in the background on a request card that code keeps; results arrive as complete messages; several requests run at once, each with its own Stop; and Morrow remembers the conversation and starts each request from it. Under [ADR-0034](../decisions/adr/ADR-0034-morrow-drafts-plans.md) (accepted 2026-10-24), it also drafts project, task and repeating-task cards that a person starts with one tap; an agent's repeating task then also passes the routine-activation review (ADR-0034 owner question 4).

Agents read, analyse and draft freely. They never change the store, spend money or message a customer without a person approving the exact change. A short-lived cloud computer with no internet crunches numbers and opens spreadsheets. Morrow is a standalone web workspace with a small home inside the Shopify admin, paid as one monthly plan on the Shopify bill, with one credit balance that is the only limit on AI spend (ADR-0039) and an extra-credit cap the Owner sets. Work is never cut short by a limit: a job that trips a safety check, needs an answer or runs out of credits pauses, keeps its place and asks a person (ADR-0041 items 3 and 9).

**Teammates** (ADR-0037, in place of ADR-0023 decision 11's specialists): every agent but Morrow is a teammate, a scoped subagent with its own installation, principal, grants, Shopify scopes, model, notebook and loop, of one of two origins: built by Morrow's team and installed in one click, or created by Morrow. Morrow uses them in any way at depth 1, any number of calls and hand-offs in one job, each one child under its envelope, admitted and checked in code on its own. Calls are asynchronous (ADR-0041 items 21 and 22): `agent.ask` returns at once and Morrow keeps working or waits with `run.wait`; it sends guidance with `agent.message`, cancels with `agent.cancel`, asks a follow-up that reopens the same teammate's context, and fans the same work out over many records with `agent.map`; at 32 children per envelope the job checks in and the requester's Continue issues a successor envelope. Teammate calls happen only in a request's own job, never in the main thread of a person's Morrow conversation (`fork_first`). A call returns a TeammateResult@1, structured and with no character cap, whose large outputs are result items Morrow opens with `memory.read`, and the result taints Morrow's job when the teammate read untrusted content. A separate review run, at the effort of the work it checks, reviews big deliverables before Morrow presents them; its verdict can only block (ADR-0041 item 19). A teammate never calls, hands off to or creates an agent. **Agent cards:** Morrow drafts a new teammate as an agent card; the model writes its name, purpose, instructions and any skill text and picks first-party skills and catalogue tools, and code derives its grants, scopes, data classes, triggers, run limits and model. A person approves the exact card ("Add to team") under the access-change review: a solo Owner types the confirmation phrase and waits out the 300-second cool-down; in a team a second member who is not the requester or an editor approves, and the Owner approves alone only while no other member holds the card's store grants (ADR-0024 owner question 1 (b)). Nobody grants beyond their own grants; the Owner requests or approves a card that writes to the store, reads orders, spends or messages customers; never `human_only` capabilities, delegation, `agents.*`, Level 2 customer data or `support.*` on a card; taint is shown and kept; Morrow may suggest removing a teammate idle for 30 days. No money cap: each teammate's run holds and draws credits like any run (ADR-0037 item 6, ADR-0039), and a call whose first hold the wallet cannot cover pauses the whole job until credits are added, instead of being refused (ADR-0041 item 9). The switches for teammate calls and agent cards stay off until G9, G10, the agent-loop switch and a passing eval round; teammate calls also wait for the separate record on in-workspace collaboration grants that the harness plan names under "Not in these PRs". **Skills:** 12 first-party job skills (`skills/<id>/SKILL.md` plus `skill.json`), pinned by release digest, which agent cards pick by id; no skill registry and no skill tool at launch.

**Models: Opus 5.5 only** (ADR-0041 item 2, amending ADR-0027 item 12 and ADR-0036 item 1; OD-2026-10-26-5 and OD-2026-10-26-7, question 1). Every agent runs Claude Opus 5.5: the Coordinator at `high` effort and every teammate of either origin (the five installed agents, Listings included, and those Morrow creates) at `medium`. The Owner may raise a single teammate to `high` (an Admin may too, for installed agents), never lower and never to another model; `xhigh` and `max` come only with a later catalogue version, after streaming and their evals pass. Haiku, Sonnet and `low` effort leave every catalogue, Listings' per-product catalogue included. A change applies from the agent's next job, that is, its next context, and never changes what the agent may do; credits follow the model's token prices.

**COD Confirmation** (ADR 0042, connected services and customer contact, approved as recommended; pull request 86, being finalised) is an installable agent that calls cash-on-delivery customers through the merchant's own calling service, under a call plan the Owner approves. It is not built now (the owner, 2026-10-26), so it is not in the table above; ADR 0042's record and plan, once merged, say what of connected services is built and when.

## Cut rules

- **G18 missed (W5)** (ADR-0037 item 9, in place of ADR-0023 decision 11's rule): if the agent-card authority packet is not merged by 2026-12-04, agent cards move to the first fast follow, 2 to 3 weeks after launch, and nothing for them starts in W7 to W10: the packet, the card and "Add to team" on the Team page, the cards' data classes and the plan's new-teammate step (follow-up PRs 3, 6, 7's card classes and 8's step). - **Slip order otherwise:** Marketing first, then the computer forecast (TypeScript fallback). Support stays unless Shopify refuses Level 2. The floor is the Coordinator, Listings, Analyst, Inventory and Support.
- **E2B not qualified at launch:** the owner chooses between the TypeScript fallbacks and holding launch. Never automatic.
- **Holiday weeks (W8 to W10):** production approvals assume the owner is available; if not, submission moves to W11 or W12 and launch to mid-March.
- **App Review objects:** under 1.2.1, remove the cause (no Stripe path from a Shopify install, matching prices, no outside-billing copy) and resubmit; under 2.2.2, grow the embedded home toward full embedding, which is a new owner decision.
