**First, v1 is built live on the owner's own store ([ADR-0035](../decisions/adr/ADR-0035-build-v1-live.md)).** The one deployed environment (the `morrow-staging-*` Workers and the Neon project `morrow-staging`) is v1 production. Work runs under a standing owner approval with a one-line activity log; staging card 2 (G6) and G15 are dropped. The order:
1. Connect the owner's store through the custom app already built.
2. Turn on the model with the owner's Anthropic key.
3. Agents draft on real products.
4. The gateway applies approved changes, with read-back and undo.
5. E2B.

## What Morrow is

Morrow is a shop's AI team, working alongside its people. A merchant installs Morrow from Shopify and picks agents from Morrow's catalogue. Each agent has a standing goal, such as "every active product has complete SEO" or "no top seller drops under 14 days of stock", and works toward it on its own: on schedules and routines, when the store changes, when a watched number moves, and in a daily look for useful work. It keeps a notebook of what it learned and plans next.

People and agents share one workspace: projects with their goals, a task board, a thread on every task, one Inbox, a shop knowledge base and an activity log. Under [ADR-0033](../decisions/adr/ADR-0033-work-model-v2.md) (accepted 2026-10-24), every goal lives in one project with a measure computed in code, and a project either has an end date or is Everyday work, one area per added agent, with Quick tasks for one-off jobs. The Coordinator ("Morrow") is the main agent and keeps the conversation (ADR-0037). It answers from knowledge, asks clarifying questions and orchestrates its teammates in any way the work needs: it calls them as tools for structured results, in sequence or in parallel and the same teammate more than once, and, outside an Ask, hands work to the teammate that must own the conversation. When no teammate covers the work, it drafts a new one as an agent card from first-party skills and the fixed tool catalogue, which a person approves; the new teammate stays on the team until a person removes it. Teammates never call, hand off to or create agents. Under [ADR-0034](../decisions/adr/ADR-0034-morrow-drafts-plans.md) (accepted 2026-10-24), it also drafts project, task and repeating-task cards that a person starts with one tap; an agent's repeating task then also passes the routine-activation review (ADR-0034 owner question 4).

Agents read, analyse and draft freely. They never change the store, spend money or message a customer without a person approving the exact change. A short-lived cloud computer with no internet crunches numbers and opens spreadsheets. Morrow is a standalone web workspace with a small home inside the Shopify admin, paid as one monthly plan on the Shopify bill, with one credit balance that is the only limit on AI spend (ADR-0039) and an extra-credit cap the Owner sets.

## Launch agents and teammates

All agents share one tool catalogue, one loop (`AgentRunWorkflow`) and one gateway. They differ only in data: manifest, skills, role prompt, tool subset, triggers, rules and evals.

| Agent | Jobs | Optional scopes | Customer data | Computer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Coordinator, "Morrow" (built in) | Answers from knowledge; orchestrates teammates for Asks and @mentions in any way the work needs, calling them as tools for structured results (in sequence or in parallel, the same one more than once) or, outside an Ask, handing work to them, at depth 1; asks clarifying questions; ranks suggestions. | Listings | Titles, descriptions, SEO, tags, alt text, collections; weekly quality sweep; batch proposals and revert | `write_files` | None | No |
| Business Analyst | Daily pulse and weekly review; monitors on sales, AOV, refunds, unfulfilled orders, stock cover and margin; investigations; read-only | `read_orders` | Level 1 only | Yes |
| Inventory and Purchasing | Days of cover; low- and dead-stock alerts; reorder suggestions with a purchase-order file and a draft supplier email the merchant sends | `read_orders` | Level 1 only | Yes, with a TypeScript fallback |

**Teammates** (ADR-0037, in place of ADR-0023 decision 11's specialists): every agent but Morrow is a teammate, a scoped subagent with its own installation, principal, grants, Shopify scopes, model, notebook and loop, of one of two origins: built by Morrow's team and installed in one click, or created by Morrow. Morrow uses them in any way at depth 1, any number of calls and hand-offs in one run, each one child under its envelope, admitted and checked in code on its own; a call returns a TeammateResult@1, structured and with no character cap, whose large outputs are result items Morrow opens page by page, and the result taints Morrow's run when the teammate read untrusted content. A teammate never calls, hands off to or creates an agent. **Agent cards:** Morrow drafts a new teammate as an agent card; the model writes its name, purpose, instructions and any skill text and picks first-party skills and catalogue tools, and code derives its grants, scopes, data classes, triggers, run limits and model. A person approves the exact card ("Add to team") under the access-change review: a solo Owner types the confirmation phrase and waits out the 300-second cool-down; in a team a second member who is not the requester or an editor approves, and the Owner approves alone only while no other member holds the card's store grants (ADR-0024 owner question 1 (b)). Nobody grants beyond their own grants; the Owner requests or approves a card that writes to the store, reads orders, spends or messages customers; never `human_only` capabilities, delegation, `agents.*`, Level 2 customer data or `support.*` on a card; taint is shown and kept; Morrow may suggest removing a teammate idle for 30 days. No money cap: each teammate's run holds and draws credits like any run (ADR-0037 item 6, ADR-0039). The switches for teammate calls and agent cards stay off until G9, G10, the agent-loop switch and a passing eval round. **Skills:** 12 first-party job skills (`skills/<id>/SKILL.md` plus `skill.json`), pinned by release digest, which agent cards pick by id; no skill registry and no skill tool at launch.

**Models** (ADR-0027 item 12): the Owner chooses each agent's model and thinking effort, Haiku, Sonnet or Opus 5.5 at low, medium or high (an Admin may too, for installed agents). By default the Coordinator runs Opus 5.5 at high effort and every teammate of either origin (the five installed agents, Listings included, and those Morrow creates) Opus 5.5 at medium (ADR-0036). A change applies from the agent's next run and never changes what the agent may do; credits follow the model's token prices.
