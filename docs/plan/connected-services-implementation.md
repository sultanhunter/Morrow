# Connected services and customer contact: implementation plan

- Record: [ADR-0042](../decisions/adr/ADR-0042-connected-services-and-customer-contact.md) (Accepted 2026-10-26, OD-2026-10-26-9), which answers the owner's question of that day (OD-2026-10-26-8). Its owner questions, all answered as recommended, are on Q-adr-0042-owner-questions.
- Written 2026-10-26 from `origin/main` at `c1709d65`, beside the harness plan (`harness-foundations-implementation.md`, for ADR-0040 and ADR-0041, accepted 2026-10-26 in OD-2026-10-26-7) and the v1 build order's PRs 1 to 26. Reordered the same day to foundation first, after the owner's answer.
- **Scope, as decided** (OD-2026-10-26-9):
  - COD Confirmation is an installable agent, Morrow's first-party package that a person adds to their workspace. It is not built now.
  - Foundation first: CS3 to CS9, CS11 and CS12 are shared foundation, built now to production grade, behind switches that stay off, and tested against CS3's fake voice provider.
  - Waiting until the owner asks for the agent: the rows written for the agent and its calling service, CS10 (the Plivo adapter, its entry record and live qualification), CS13, CS14 (the contact screens), CS15 (the qualification scripts and the COD rounds) and CS16's contact switches (CS16b). Which calling service the shop uses is still open (question 4).
  - The general foundation for installable agents that use live store data and scheduled actions is question 17's own change, ADR 0043 (store events, schedules and standing action policies), in pull request 88 (branch `claude/store-data-foundation-records`, plan `docs/plan/store-data-foundation-implementation.md`). The lead reads the owner's scope as making it foundation work now; the owner may correct that reading (OD-2026-10-26-9). ADR 0043 covers orders, products and stock, with Level 1 data for the runs it starts; customer-level data for agents other than Support, the other half of the owner's "orders/customers", needs a further record of its own.
  - The P09.T05 customer-data deletion path is built first, as foundation (OD-2026-10-26-10), by SD4 of the store-data foundation plan.
- Rules (CLAUDE.md):
  - each PR is small, behind a switch committed off, on a short-lived `claude/` branch, with its tests;
  - each PR gets one AI review (separate context, same model family), with the security lens for credentials, provider adapters, authority, RLS, migrations, approvals, budgets and durable execution;
  - stacks are at most two PRs deep;
  - sizes: S is a day or less, M is two to three days, L is a week (usually two PRs).
- Every class's deletion handlers, clock and S8 rows land in the PR that first writes it (ADR-0031 item 4; ADR-0042 item 24). CS3 to CS8 do not depend on the agent loop. The contact path (CS8 to CS12) runs no model.
- The owner answered every question as recommended, so the rows below stand. Two parts of question 4 stay open until the owner asks for the agent: which calling service and which WhatsApp provider the shop uses.
