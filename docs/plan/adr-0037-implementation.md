## Not in these PRs

Each needs its own owner decision first (ADR-0037, Consequences): Level 2 customer data or `support.*` for workspace-created agents; more than one teammate call per Morrow run; a fold or split of first-party agents; Morrow reading store data; per-person memory; the notebook read-back path; caching the conversation; compaction; an on-demand `skill.read`; a lighter review gate for "Add to team" than ADR-0011's access-change rule.
