## State, release and cleanup-only custody

Track acquiring→connecting→attributing→open/one_query_pending→releasing→closed with a separate sticky held bit. Allocate and retain handles before awaits. Never expose a primary until a source-fixed self-attribution SELECT has returned one valid own backend tuple under live original control. A client in releasing/closed never accepts owner SQL.

release(discard) publishes exactly one promise before any end/close/broker callback can reenter; repeated calls join it. Every dedicated connection is disposable. release(true) is also used after normal COMMIT and must not itself mark operation failure. Do not add business COMMIT/ROLLBACK during release. Normal success requires the one-shot primary-close→observer-read→observer-close→lease-ack sequence below and the unchanged owner's final freshness check.

First release/stop latches one independent runtime cleanup-only waiting ceiling C=first release/stop runtime time+1000ms maximum, never renewed by late events. Normal release remains subject to original D/live authority; C is not a new business/observer deadline. After hold/authority loss, only already-owned object disposal and once-only lease release may run. No new observer, borrow, attribution/metadata query or business SQL. Late callbacks retain once-only best-effort sinks; cleanup-only completion after C is late/unverified and never promotes operation success. C bounds waiting, not physical completion of uncancellable SDK/broker promises. Invalid business clock cannot be replaced by the cleanup clock.

Primary self-attribution must validate one row containing actual positive PID, backend_start, current database, current_user/session_user and matching pg_stat_activity role/database/application_name. Compare it in SQL through a validated timestamptz parameter.

Normal positive release is exactly:

1. Close/discard primary and acknowledge its actual transport close within live normal authority. SDK end alone does not establish fresh backend absence.
2. Recheck original D/signal, lease expiry/revocation and held bit; allocate at most one same-role/database/host SDK observer under that same lease. No observer is created after abort/expiry/revocation/poison.
3. Submit one source-fixed fresh autocommit SELECT. Require exactly one valid self row, equal positive self/backend PID, expected self role/database/name and canonical six-digit timestamp before considering primary_present=false. No self row/null/hidden/multiple/malformed fields means held, never absence.
4. A matching primary still present, uncertainty, error, timeout or revocation holds without polling/retry. Do not send cancellation/termination SQL. Positive live-primary same-role visibility is a required lab control; malformed/truncated/wrong namespace attribution cannot supply an absence verdict.
5. Close observer transport once and acknowledge closure within D; no recursive observer or observer business SQL. This is transport closure, not fresh proof of its own backend absence.
6. Release the one lease once and require timely acknowledgement within normal authority. Password-release acknowledgement is custody evidence, not remote password/session invalidation. Resolve only if every normal predicate stayed valid and no sticky held occurred.

Missing primary attribution means close-only cleanup; do not discover or adopt guessed backends later. If authority ends after observer work already started, stop further SQL and dispose known handles only.
