# Connected services and customer contact: sources

The sources behind [ADR-0042](../decisions/adr/ADR-0042-connected-services-and-customer-contact.md) (Proposed), from the four research reports of 2026-10-26: the repository, platform practice, the technical and market report, and the legal report. Each entry names the source, what it supports, how it was read, and where ADR-0042 uses it.

How to read the entries:

- **Read** means the page or text was read in the session. **Summary** means the claim rests on a search summary, a snippet or a secondary source, and the primary text was not read. **Unconfirmed** marks a claim the report itself could not confirm. ADR-0042 keeps each claim at the strength given here.
- Quotations are short phrases only. Prices, rates and rules change; each is as read on 2026-10-26.
- This is research, not legal advice. Counsel confirms the law before any outside merchant (ADR-0042 item 13, gate G16).
- The three reviews of the draft (security, legal and privacy, product) and the review of pull request 86 produced findings, not new sources. A few claims in ADR-0042 came from the reviews without a source in these reports; they are listed at the end, to be sourced before anyone relies on them.

## 1. Cash on delivery, Shopify and the providers

### Shopify

- [Shopify developer community: identifying a COD order](https://community.shopify.dev/t/how-to-identify-order-is-cod-or-not-using-admin-graphql-api-of-get-order/32197). Read; a community answer, not documentation. Supports: the Order object has no COD flag; a Shopify staff reply recommends checking the order's transactions for the COD gateway with `manualPaymentGateway` true; display names can be renamed; bank deposits and money orders also report `manualPaymentGateway` true; a non-staff reply says third-party COD apps may not set it. ADR-0042: Context, item 18 (the two-step COD test and the Owner's COD list).
- [Admin GraphQL `OrderTransaction`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/OrderTransaction) and [`Order`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/Order). Read. Supports: the `gateway`, `formattedGateway` and `manualPaymentGateway` fields; `paymentGatewayNames`; `Order.phone` is null when no phone was given at checkout. ADR-0042: items 11 (the phone order of preference, `no_phone`) and 18.
- [`OrderDisplayFinancialStatus`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-07/enums/OrderDisplayFinancialStatus). Read. Supports: the closed list of statuses, with no stated mapping for COD, so it is tested on a development store. ADR-0042: item 18; plan CS8.
- [Webhook reference, `orders/create` sample payload](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/webhooks?reference=toml). Read. Supports: the payload carries the customer, the shipping and billing addresses (each with a phone), a top-level phone, `payment_gateway_names`, `financial_status`, the note and the tags; order topics need `read_orders`. ADR-0042: items 7 (phones transit the connector unread) and 18 (the minimiser).
- [Ignore duplicate webhooks](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks/ignore-duplicates) and [HTTPS webhook delivery](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/webhooks/subscribe/https). Read. Supports: deduplication on `X-Shopify-Webhook-Id`, not the event id; 8 retries over 4 hours; HMAC-SHA256 over the raw body with a timing-safe compare; a reconciliation job is recommended. ADR-0042: Context, item 7, item 18 (the missed-order sweep).
- [Protected customer data](https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/launch/protected-customer-data). Read. Supports: Phone is a Level 2 field requested on its own; public apps are reviewed; unapproved fields are redacted; Level 2 adds encrypted backups, separate test and production data, limited staff access, an access log and an incident-response policy. ADR-0042: Context, item 15.
- [`tagsAdd`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/mutations/tagsAdd) and [`orderUpdate`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/mutations/orderUpdate). Read. Supports: `tagsAdd` appends to an order's tags while setting `tags` overwrites them; order updates need `write_orders`. Whether `tagsAdd` on an order needs exactly `write_orders` is confirmed in plan CS11. ADR-0042: item 19.
- [`orderCancel`](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/mutations/orderCancel). Read. Supports: it needs `write_orders`, returns a job to follow, cannot be undone, defaults `notifyCustomer` to false and caps the staff note at 255 characters. ADR-0042: item 20.
- [Shopify API terms](https://www.shopify.com/legal/api-terms). Summary; clause text not read. Supports: minimum data; data goes to third parties only to run the app's services or with the merchant's express authorisation; no AI training on merchant or customer data without written consent. ADR-0042: items 1 and 15, and counsel's question on a kept suppression digest (item 13).

### Apps merchants already use

- [Cashify COD Order Confirmation](https://apps.shopify.com/cod-order-confirmation). Read. Supports: IVR calls and SMS or OTP, with pending, confirmed and cancelled tags; charges per received call; mixed reviews. ADR-0042: Context (the market).
- [Hillteck COD Order Verification](https://apps.shopify.com/cod-order-verification) and [Hillteck's IVR and WhatsApp article](https://hillteck.com/blog/cod-verification-ivr-vs-whatsapp.html). Read. Supports: IVR calls and WhatsApp messages that label orders; the flow of a call within about a minute, WhatsApp buttons a few minutes later, then manual review or automatic cancellation. ADR-0042: Context, item 17 (the likely WhatsApp order).
- [Tokki](https://apps.shopify.com/tokki), [SoniQA](https://apps.shopify.com/soniqa) and [KODIAI](https://apps.shopify.com/kodiai-verify-cod-orders). Read. Supports: newer AI-voice apps with no reviews yet; one calls after a failed delivery rather than before dispatch. ADR-0042: Context.
- [Confirmify](https://apps.shopify.com/confirmify), [Level](https://apps.shopify.com/level), [CODVerifyAPP](https://apps.shopify.com/codverifyapp) and [Cartsaver OTP CoD](https://apps.shopify.com/cartsaver-otp-cod). Summary; listings not fetched. Supports: WhatsApp-button confirmation with tags, some with automatic cancellation. ADR-0042: Context.

### Whether calls reduce returns

- [ProfitBox360 on COD and prepaid RTO in India](https://www.profitbox360.com/research/india/ecommerce/cod-prepaid-rto-rates-india), [Qikink on return to origin](https://qikink.com/blog/what-is-return-to-origin-how-it-affects-online-businesses/) and [Pragma on reducing RTO](https://www.bepragma.ai/blogs/how-to-reduce-rto-in-indian-e-commerce-without-hurting-cod-orders). Read; secondary and vendor data. Supports: COD RTO of roughly 24 to 35% against roughly 1.5 to 8% for prepaid; COD is most orders. ADR-0042: Context.
- [GoKwik's 2023 results, as reported](https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/gokwik-helps-ecommerce-brands-save-over-130-cr-worth-of-rto-losses-in-2023). Read; vendor data with no control group. Supports: RTO reductions from mixed interventions, not calls alone. ADR-0042: Context, item 25 (measure, don't assume).
- [Hillteck](https://hillteck.com/blog/cod-verification-ivr-vs-whatsapp.html), [Acefone](https://www.acefone.com/blog/cod-confirmation-automation-how-ai-voice-bots-cut-rto-before-your-orders-leave-the-warehouse/) and [Moreno and Ngwe, HBS working paper 19-030](https://hbswk.hbs.edu/item/opportunistic-returns-and-dynamic-pricing-empirical-evidence-from-online-retailing-in-emerging-markets). Read. Supports: vendor claims of 15 to 45% fewer RTOs without methodology; no independent controlled study found; buyers who expect to return choose COD more often, which supports targeting but does not test confirmation calls. ADR-0042: Context, item 25 (the holdout; no vendor numbers in product copy).

### Calling providers

- [Plivo: make a call](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice/api/call/make-a-call). Read. Supports: `answer_url` returning Plivo XML, `hangup_url`, `ring_url`, `time_limit` and `ring_timeout`; a `request_uuid` in the response; callbacks with the call id, status and event; a call-details read; no documented idempotency key. ADR-0042: items 6 (a per-call token for correlation; `uncertain` terminal) and 12 (the call state machine).
- [Plivo: signature validation](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice/concepts/signature-validation.md). Read. Supports: V3 signatures, HMAC-SHA256 with the auth token over the URL, the sorted parameters and a nonce. ADR-0042: item 7.
- [Plivo India voice pricing](https://www.plivo.com/voice/pricing/in/). Read. Supports: domestic calls at ₹0.38 a minute with a 30-second pulse and about ₹200 a month for a number, as read; other Plivo pages show other rates. ADR-0042: item 23, Consequences.
- [Plivo: calling in India for voice agents](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice-agents/sip-trunking/deploy/calling-in-india.md) and [Plivo: India calling](https://www.plivo.com/docs/voice/concepts/india-calling). Read; Plivo's own summary of TRAI's rules, not checked against the Gazette. Supports: India call traffic must be "anchored on servers located in India"; an India data-region account and KYC; Vapi listed as not supported; number series by call type; transactional calls within 30 minutes need no explicit consent. ADR-0042: Context, items 2, 4 and 13.
- [Exotel: outgoing call to a flow](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/voice-v1/api-reference/outgoing-call-to-flow), [status callback](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/voice-v1/api-reference/status-callback), [webhooks](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/references/webhooks) and [voicebot applet](https://developer.exotel.com/docs/agentstream/stream-voicebot-applet). Read. Supports: a transactional call type; callbacks with no signature that may be delayed, duplicated or lost, with the call-details read as the fallback; a streaming voicebot. Exotel publishes no per-minute rates. ADR-0042: Context, item 3 (`hint_then_read`), Alternatives.
- Knowlarity, through [Gupshup's documentation](https://docs.gupshup.io/docs/make-a-call). Summary; no official Knowlarity documentation found. Supports: an agent-first click-to-call shape only. Unconfirmed. ADR-0042: not used.
- [Twilio: India voice guidelines](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/guidelines/in/voice). Read. Supports: outbound calls to India only "from international (non-Indian) numbers"; consent required. ADR-0042: Context, Alternatives.
- [Twilio: India](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/voice/pricing/in) and [US](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/voice/pricing/us) voice pricing, and [ConversationRelay](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/twiml/connect/conversationrelay) with its [voice configuration](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/conversationrelay/voice-configuration). Read. Supports: per-minute rates to India; a streaming AI voice option with a Hindi default. ADR-0042: Alternatives.
- [Twilio: the call resource](https://www.twilio.com/docs/voice/api/call-resource). Summary; unconfirmed. Supports: a default of one outbound call a second per account. ADR-0042: item 8 (one a second until the entry says otherwise).
- [Bolna: make a call](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/api-reference/calls/make.md), [call status webhooks](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/polling-call-status-webhooks.md), [pricing](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/pricing) and [Indian server configuration](https://www.bolna.ai/docs/enterprise/indian-server-configuration). Read. Supports: no documented idempotency key; unsigned webhooks; India residency only on Enterprise and with Plivo telephony. ADR-0042: Alternatives.
- [Vapi: create a call](https://docs.vapi.ai/api-reference/calls/create), [server events](https://docs.vapi.ai/server-url/events), [server authentication](https://docs.vapi.ai/server-url/server-authentication) and [pricing](https://vapi.ai/pricing). Read. Supports: a free-form AI voice platform with its own transcripts and recordings; no credentials sent when the server address comes from an inline configuration. ADR-0042: Context, Alternatives.
- [Retell: create a phone call](https://docs.retellai.com/api-references/create-phone-call), [webhooks](https://docs.retellai.com/features/webhook), [secure webhooks](https://docs.retellai.com/features/secure-webhook), [post-call analysis](https://docs.retellai.com/features/post-call-analysis-overview) and [pricing](https://www.retellai.com/pricing). Read. Supports: an idempotency key on call creation; numbers bought from Retell dial only US destinations; post-call extraction is an LLM reading the transcript, so its outcome is untrusted. ADR-0042: Context, items 3 (free-text features off) and 6, Alternatives.
- [ElevenLabs Agents: outbound call](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/api-reference/sip-trunk/outbound-call.md), [post-call webhooks](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/agents-platform/workflows/post-call-webhooks), [pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/agents) and [India data residency](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/introducing-india-data-residency). Read. Supports: a free-form AI voice with transcripts and audio in webhooks; India residency only on Enterprise. ADR-0042: Alternatives.

### WhatsApp

- [Message template guidelines](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/message-templates/guidelines/). Read. Supports: outside the customer-service window only templates can be sent, each approved first and categorised as authentication, marketing or utility. ADR-0042: item 17.
- [Template categorisation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-categorization.md). Read. Supports: an order confirmation is a utility example; mixed content with a promotion is marketing, and a utility submission judged marketing is approved as marketing. ADR-0042: items 9 (no promotional purposes) and 17.
- [Meta: WhatsApp pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing), with third-party figures from [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026) and [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-business-pricing/). - [Create a webhook endpoint](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/create-webhook-endpoint.md), [set up webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api/guides/set-up-webhooks) and [button messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/messages/button.md). Read. Supports: the GET verification handshake; `X-Hub-Signature-256` over the body; retries for up to 7 days; a button tap carries its payload and the original message id. ADR-0042: item 17.
- [Business-initiated WhatsApp calls](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/calling/business-initiated-calls.md), with [Infobip](https://support.infobip.com/docs/whatsapp/whatsapp-business-calling/business-initiated-calling) and [Chakra](https://chakrahq.com/article/whatsapp-cloud-api-calling-feature-details/amp/). Read; India availability claimed only by third parties. Supports: calls need the user's prior permission. ADR-0042: not used; a later option.

## 2. Law in India, the US, the EU and the UK

### India: telecom rules

- [TRAI TCCCPR amendment of 12 February 2025](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/Regulation_12022025.pdf). Read. Supports: a transactional call answers a customer-initiated transaction within thirty minutes and needs no explicit consent; promotional content in any commercial call makes it promotional; transactional and service calls are not UCC, yet commercial communication from an unregistered sender is treated as UCC; Schedule IV's signals for suspected telemarketers; barring for promotional calls from service series. ADR-0042: Context, items 9 and 13.
- [TRAI TCCCPR Third Amendment, notification 05 of 2026 (18 September 2026)](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/Regulation_18092026.pdf) and [press release 119 of 2026](https://www.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-09/PR_No119of2026.pdf). Read. Supports: the two kinds of service call, one giving "information regarding delivery of goods" with no consent and one facilitating "the ongoing purchase" with explicit consent for 7 days; A2P calls, AI voices included, declared with their caller-ID ranges and recorded on DLT, or treated as UCC; AI and ML spam flagging, with complaint-based action at 3 complaints in 10 days on a flagged number; the regulations do not exempt a sender from the DPDP Act; commencement 30 and 60 days after Gazette publication, whose date is unconfirmed. ADR-0042: Context, item 13 (the two policy modes, attestations, the connection stop).
- [TRAI consolidated regulations of 21 May 2026](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-05/CA_21052026.pdf). Read; the time-band mapping is inferred from row order. Supports: operators report auto-dialler senders above 3% abandoned or 1% silent calls over 24 hours; transactional and service calls are not blocked in any time band. ADR-0042: item 13 (the connection stop; hours of 09:00 to 21:00).
- TRAI press releases on number series: [11 of 2025](https://cms.trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-02/PR_No.11of2025.pdf), [91 of 2026](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-07/PR_No91of2026.pdf) and [113 of 2026](https://trai.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/PR_No113of2026_1.pdf). Read in part; some scanned. Supports: 140 for promotional calls, 1600 for regulated financial entities and government, 1601 for other service calls with phase I limited to utilities and logistics; e-commerce not yet covered. ADR-0042: item 13 (counsel's question on registration and series).
- [Secondary coverage of the Third Amendment](https://foxmandal.in/?p=60468), with [IndiaLaw](https://indialaw.in/blog/food/trai-anti-spam-rules/) and [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/amp/business/trai-tightens-anti-spam-rules-brings-ai-based-detection-automated-calls-under-framework-1988473). Summary. Supports: the same A2P declaration and terminating charge, as read by others. ADR-0042: Context.
- [DoT circular 01/2026 on CNAP](https://www.dot.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/10/d86da4092d50f052becd6113de83d37e.pdf). Summary; not read in full. Supports: calling-name display of the KYC name from 1 November 2026, on by default. ADR-0042: Context, item 13 (the KYC-name attestation).
- [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020](https://consumeraffairs.nic.in/theconsumerprotection/consumer-protection-e-commerce-rules-2020) and [the draft 2024 guidelines on unsolicited business communication](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/government-invites-public-feedback-on-draft-guidelines-for-prevention-and-regulation-of-unsolicited-and-unwarranted-business-communication-2024). Not read; unconfirmed. Supports: questions for counsel only.

### India: data protection

- [The DPDP Act 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf). Read. Supports: section 7(a)'s legitimate use for a purpose the person gave the data for, with Illustration (I), a purchase receipt sent to the phone given; consent rules for promotion; the fiduciary answers for its processor and needs a contract; rights of access, correction and erasure. ADR-0042: Context, items 1 (roles) and 13 (counsel's questions).
- [DPDP Rules 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) and [the commencement notification](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf). Read. Supports: notice contents; security safeguards and processor terms (Rule 6); breach notice (Rule 7); at least one year of logs (Rule 8(3)); core duties 18 months after publication, about 13 May 2027 (a computed date). ADR-0042: Context, item 24 (the 90-day clock is a maximum; counsel decides before May 2027).
- [JSA on the DPDP Rules](https://www.jsalaw.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JSA-Prism-InfoTech-November-2025-DPDP-Rules.Final_.pdf) and [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/centre-notifies-dpdp-rules-implementation-planned-in-phases-spread-over-12-18-months-3798465). Summary. Supports: the notification date and the phased start. ADR-0042: Context.
- No source was found for a rule on call recording by non-financial businesses. ADR-0042: item 15 (no recording in v1).

### United States

- [47 CFR 64.1200 (eCFR, 1 October 2026)](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-01/title-47.xml?part=64&section=64.1200). Read. Supports: prior express consent for artificial-voice calls to mobiles; written consent for telemarketing; the business's registered name and a callback number at the start of every artificial-voice message; opt-out and revocation duties; calling hours of 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.; a 3% abandonment limit for telemarketing. ADR-0042: Context, items 11 (the US identification fields) and 13.
- [FCC 24-17](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf), with [the FCC's announcement](https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-confirms-tcpa-applies-ai-technologies-generate-human-voices) and [Wilson Sonsini's analysis](https://wsgr.com/en/insights/fcc-rules-ai-generated-voices-are-artificial-under-the-tcpa.html). Read. Supports: AI-generated voices are artificial voices under the TCPA. ADR-0042: Context.
- [FCC 26-67](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-26-67A1.pdf). Read; effective date unconfirmed. Supports: revocation scoped to a category of informational calls. ADR-0042: counsel's questions when the US opens.
- [FCC 24-84](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-84A1.pdf), [FCC 12-21](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-12-21A1.pdf) and [an FCC fact sheet](https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DOC-404036A1.pdf). Summary. Supports: a proposed AI-disclosure rule, not found adopted; informational calls need only prior express consent. ADR-0042: counsel's questions.
- [16 CFR 310, the Telemarketing Sales Rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/api/versioner/v1/full/2026-10-01/title-16.xml?part=310). Read. Supports: an upsell is a separate telemarketing transaction. ADR-0042: items 9 and Alternatives (no upsell or prepaid offer).
- [McLaughlin Chiropractic v. McKesson (2025)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-1226_1a72.pdf). Summary. Supports: FCC interpretations do not bind district courts. ADR-0042: background only.
- State laws, statutory damages and recording consent. Not fetched; unconfirmed leads for counsel.

### European Union

- [EU AI Act, Article 50 (AI Act Service Desk)](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-50), with [a mirror of the text](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/) and [Jones Walker's note](https://www.joneswalker.com/en/insights/blogs/ai-law-blog/yes-august-2-still-matters-the-eu-approved-a-high-risk-ai-delay-but-most-trans.html). ADR-0042: items 1 and 20 (cancellation stays a person's decision).

### United Kingdom

- [ICO: telephone marketing under PECR](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/guide-to-pecr/electronic-and-telephone-marketing/telephone-marketing/). ## 3. Integration patterns

### Credentials and connections

- [MCP authorization (2025-11-25)](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/authorization) and [MCP security best practices](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-06-18/basic/security_best_practices). Read. Supports: no token passthrough; PKCE; no tokens in query strings; minimal scopes; SSRF through metadata addresses. ADR-0042: items 2 (no merchant-supplied servers) and 7 (no query strings).
- [MCP elicitation](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/client/elicitation). Read. Supports: servers must not collect passwords, API keys or tokens through a form the client or model sees. ADR-0042: Context, item 4 (sealed entry).
- [Invariant Labs on tool poisoning](https://invariantlabs.ai/blog/mcp-security-notification-tool-poisoning-attacks). Read. Supports: hidden instructions in tool descriptions, descriptions changed after approval, and one server redirecting another's mail. ADR-0042: item 2 (catalogue entries only, pinned by digest).
- [Cloudflare's OAuth provider for Workers](https://github.com/cloudflare/workers-oauth-provider) and [Cloudflare MCP authorization](https://developers.cloudflare.com/agents/model-context-protocol/authorization/). Read. Supports: secrets stored only as hashes; the Worker issues its own token, separate from the upstream one. ADR-0042: item 7 (tokens stored as hashes).
- [Auth0 Token Vault](https://auth0.com/ai/docs/intro/call-third-party-apis-on-users-behalf.md) and [Arcade's secret injection pattern](https://www.arcade.dev/patterns/secret-injection). Read. Supports: never accept credentials as tool parameters; inject them server-side. ADR-0042: Context, items 4 and 21 (no tool names a credential).
- [OWASP secrets management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Secrets_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html), [HashiCorp Vault transit](https://developer.hashicorp.com/vault/docs/secrets/transit) and [Cloudflare Secrets Store](https://developers.cloudflare.com/secrets-store/).
