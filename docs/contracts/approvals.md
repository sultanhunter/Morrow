# Exact proposal approvals

Version: 1.0.0 candidate. Owner: Morrow platform owner. Review: Codex semantic self-review plus schema/fixture checks; integration qualification remains required. Canonical plan sequence: 13. Consumer: P11.

## Shape and ownership

Schemas: Proposal, Approval, CrossHostResume. Exact field types, nullability, required properties and closed object validation are in schemas/v1/core.schema.json; capability bodies are in capabilities.schema.json. State actors and guards are in state-transitions.json. Representative valid/invalid fixtures are in fixtures/P01.A02.json. Payload references are immutable scope/revision/digest bindings, not storage URLs or bearer credentials.

## Command and query semantics

Compute proposal digest from canonical content using a RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization Scheme ([specification](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785/)) and SHA-256. Bind resource/account/field/parameter/cost/prior-version/review-policy contents. Decisions identify reviewer and current eligibility, exact digest, scope digest and expiry. Selected-item approval creates an exact selected change set. Changes invalidate review. Approval itself requires an eligible decision grant, not a recursively approved approval. Execution still needs an independent eligible grant and current provider authority.

## Failure invariant

Wrong revision, changed ceiling, replayed handoff, expired review or ineligible reviewer blocks dispatch. Failures use ErrorEnvelope and must not leak inaccessible content. Current policy and resource checks apply to all reads, mutations and derived artifacts.

## Compatibility and evidence

Every command/read declares schema version 1. Unknown fields and invalid enum values fail validation; no implicit coercion/default authority. Breaking fields/states/meaning require a new version and explicit migration with retained workflow histories. Additions remain opt-in via negotiated schema versions, not silently accepted fields. Consumers pin reviewed contract revisions. Run npm run verify:contracts and npm run test:contracts for contract fixture evidence; these do not prove production authorization or provider access. Open provider/region/retention/billing targets remain in the decision register and block consumers that need them.

## Contract v1.1: kill switches (P01.T02a PR-A)

ADR-0005 makes five capabilities kill switches: `ads.campaign.pause`, `agents.installation.disable`, `governance.membership.revoke`, `governance.connection.disconnect` and `agents.release.revoke`. Each is `human_only`, with `review_required: false`, so it executes without a second approver and creates no Approval. It still needs a current eligible human grant for `execute`. Its v1.1 input carries a `KillSwitchControl`: a reason with at least one non-whitespace character, and an optional `sourceProposalRef`. The human evaluator returns the `kill_switch_record` obligation in place of `exact_approval`: the effect commits with an append-only `KillSwitchRecord`, and the owner notification derives from that record (P05.T01z, P11.T05). An agent may propose a stop. A human executing it from that proposal passes the proposal as `sourceProposalRef`, which is rechecked for the same tenant, the same capability and covering targets, and confers nothing (`packages/policy/src/kill-switch.ts`).

Reversals keep review: unpause (`ads.campaign.activate`), re-enable (`agents.installation.activate`), reconnect (`governance.connection.connect`), re-invite (`governance.membership.invite`), re-grant (`governance.grants.revise`) and republish (`agents.release.publish`) still require `exact_approval`. The review policy (ReviewPolicy, Proposal@2, Approval@2) is PR-B under ADR-0011, below. Versioning is described in contract-versions.md.

## Contract v1.1: review policy and exact proposals (P01.T02a PR-B)

ADR-0011 makes ADR-0005 §3 exact. The contracts are core v1.1 `Proposal` (Proposal@2) and `Approval` (Approval@2), both declared replacements of the v1 shapes, plus `ReviewPolicy`, `ReviewPolicyRules`, `ReviewRule`, `RestrictedReviewRule`, `ApprovalDecision`, `ReviewDecisionInput`, `ProposalTarget`, `TargetSelection` and the ceiling types. The rules live in the pure review library `packages/policy/src/review.ts` (`@morrow/policy/review`), which P11.T01 imports; it does no I/O and reads no clock.

- **Exact proposals.** The server builds every proposal. `risk` comes from the fixed effect-to-risk table in `review-policy-defaults.json` (ADR-0011 Q9); `irreversible` is the catalogue flag (Q4); `proposedBy`, `requestedBy` (the task initiator or mandate owner, always a human), `beneficiaryPrincipalIds` and `contextTaint` equal trusted facts; `reviewPolicyRef` is the workspace's single current policy (`storeId` null). `checkProposal` gives each mismatch its own code, and `TENANT_MISMATCH` for any reference outside the trusted organization, workspace and proposal store. Each target carries `maximumCredits` (integer string) and `maximumCost` (non-negative money or null); the totals are their exact BigInt sums, and the digest is SHA-256 of the RFC 8785 canonical proposal without `proposalDigest`, so every field is bound.
- **Review rules.** `ReviewPolicy.rules` holds a rule per risk class plus an `irreversible` overlay merged field by field, taking the stricter value (`effectiveRule`). Communication, financial, access_change and irreversible rules can never allow self-approval. Defaults (seeded by P11.T01 as revision 1): internal and external allow self-approval, the restricted classes use solo-owner confirmation; quorum 1; pending 7 d, 72 h and 24 h; approved validity 24 h, 24 h and 1 h; a 300 s cool-down and a 24 h quiet period.
- **Reviewers.** A reviewer is a human member holding `approve` on `governance.approval.decide` and `approve` on the proposal's capability for every decided target (`reviewerEligible`), inside the rule's reviewer list when it is set. The proposer, the requester and every beneficiary are conflicted: they pass only under `allowed`, or as the solo owner with the exact typed phrase. Any eligible reviewer may reject.
- **Solo owner.** The designated owner (the ADR-0009 owner record), when the only active human member and outside the quiet period after any membership change, may approve their own restricted-class proposal by typing `approve N change(s) for C credits` (plus ` and A CUR` with a cost). The approval takes effect after the cool-down, can be cancelled until then, and is rechecked at `effectiveAt` and at dispatch (`SOLO_OWNER_STATUS_LOST`).
- **Partial approval.** A reviewer may approve a strict subset of an `allowed` proposal's targets. `previewSelection` (also `governance.approval.preview`) returns the deterministic derived proposal, with re-summed ceilings, inherited timestamps and expiry, `derivedFrom` and a new digest; the decision binds that digest. A family (root plus derivations) has at most one approved member; approving one invalidates every other pending member.
- **Decisions.** `foldDecisions` is idempotent per (`commandId`, `requestDigest`): a replay returns the recorded result, the same command with another digest gives `COMMAND_CONFLICT`, and a reviewer decides once. `requiredApprovals` distinct approvals approve; `effectiveAt` is the last decision, or later after a solo cool-down. Pending approvals expire at `min(proposal.expiresAt, createdAt + pendingExpirySeconds)` and approved ones at `min(proposal.expiresAt, effectiveAt + approvedValiditySeconds)`. `dispatchCheck` refuses an approval that is not yet effective, expired, bound to a superseded policy, or whose solo status is lost. An unknown commit is read back by `commandId`, never resubmitted.
- **Policy revisions.** `governance.review_policy.revise` is a reviewed access_change; a new revision makes approvals bound to the old one stale (`REVIEW_POLICY_NOT_CURRENT`).

Errors map to existence-free ErrorEnvelopes (`reviewError`). State transitions are the v1.1 rows in state-transitions.json; the v1 `exact_digest_scope_and_current_policy` approval rows are superseded in 1.1.
