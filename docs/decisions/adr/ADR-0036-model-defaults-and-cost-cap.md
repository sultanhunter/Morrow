# ADR-0036 Model defaults and the AI cost cap: Opus 5.5 by default for every agent, and no unrecovered-cost cap

- Status: Accepted 2026-10-25 (the owner, OD-2026-10-25-1 and OD-2026-10-25-2).
- Date: 2026-10-25
- Supersedes: none.
- Amends: ADR-0027 item 12 (the installed agents' default) and item 6 (the unrecovered-cost cap for loop runs); ADR-0022 item 11's failed-products bullet and its verify sentence (the US$4.50 unrecovered-cost cap), item 16's default (Sonnet 5.5 at low for Listings) and default member ceiling, and its owner-confirmed values 2, 6 (the cap) and 7 (the ceiling's default); ADR-0026 item 4's failed-runs bullet, and its look-for-work cap of 1% of the monthly grant per pass with its arithmetic (replaced by item 7's US$1.12 per agent per day), with RateCard@1's `caps.lookForWorkPassBasisPoints`; ADR-0027 item 12's look-for-work hold (its 1% cap); ADR-0034's "Does not change" note that the installed agents keep Sonnet 5.5 at low (OD-2026-10-23-8 answer 8); ADR-0023 decision 7 as ADR-0027 item 12 restated it.
- Does not change: the per-agent choice of ADR-0027 item 12 and who makes it; `governance.model_default.revise` stays `human_only`; the per-run US$7 and per-day US$25 backstops (ADR-0026 item 4), which the owner reconfirmed (OD-2026-10-25-2); one read-only look-for-work pass per agent a day (ADR-0024 item 6); a specialist's 5% and 20% caps; credit holds and reservations; the three-failure breaker; the Anthropic Console monthly limit; every authority rule; catalogue version 2's other values.
- Unblocks: loop runs at Opus 5.5 before billing (track T4); Listings at the new default; look-for-work passes at the installed default (build order PR 21).
- Brief: `docs/briefs/adr-0036-model-defaults-cost-cap.md` (the change on `main`, and the same change for the agent-loop branch).

## Context

ADR-0027 item 12 set the defaults: the Coordinator on Opus 5.5 at high, specialists on Opus 5.5 at medium, the five installed agents on Sonnet 5.5 at low. Listings' per-product runs (`listings-run@1`) default to Sonnet 5.5 at low through catalogue version 1 (ADR-0022 item 16). ADR-0022 item 11 caps each workspace's unrecovered cost, the AI cost of products and runs that earned no credit, at US$4.50 a billing period, and ADR-0027 item 6 counts every loop run toward it, at its hold, before the rate card. An Opus 5.5 run at medium or high holds US$6.40 to US$6.72 on its own, and the Coordinator's US$3.84 leaves no room for a second run, so the cap blocks Opus runs before billing exists. The lead put both points to the owner, who answered on 2026-10-25 (OD-2026-10-25-1); the lead confirmed its reading below to the owner in chat. Carrying it out raised three more questions, which the owner answered the same day (OD-2026-10-25-2): the default member ceiling that admits Listings' new default (item 2), the look-for-work cap, which at 1% of the monthly grant admits no pass at the new installed default (item 7), and whether the two remaining backstops stay (item 4).

## Decision

1. **Defaults.** Morrow, the main agent (the Coordinator), runs Opus 5.5 at `high`. Every other agent kind runs Opus 5.5 at `medium`: specialists, sub-agents and every installed agent (Listings, Business Analyst, Inventory, Marketing and Support). Catalogue version 2's `default_profiles.installed` becomes Opus 5.5 at `medium`. The Owner (an Admin, for installed agents) still picks each agent's model and effort from Haiku, Sonnet and Opus 5.5 at `low`, `medium` and `high`; a stored choice is kept.
2. **Listings' per-product default: catalogue version 3.** A published version never changes (ADR-0022 item 16), so version 3 publishes on this decision and version 1 retires. Version 3 is version 1 with three changes: the default selection is Opus 5.5 at `medium` (4 credits a product); the default member ceiling is 4, so it admits that default as version 1's ceiling of 1 admitted Sonnet 5.5 at `low` (the ceiling stays exactly the default's credits, and only `governance.model_default.revise` raises a workspace's; the owner confirmed this value, OD-2026-10-25-2); and it has no unrecovered-cost cap. A run recorded at version 1 is still booked at version 1. Migration 0051 seeds version 3 in SQL and retires version 1.
3. **No unrecovered-cost cap.** The US$4.50 cap is removed for Listings and for loop runs. Run admission and the per-product step no longer refuse or stop with `unrecovered_cost_cap`: migration 0051 revises `morrow_billing.unrecovered_allows` to compare with no cap, for every version, and the TypeScript twin `unrecoveredAllows` is removed. The code stays in the closed enums for runs that already ended with it. Failed products still cost no credits, and the ledger (`unrecovered_costs`, `unrecovered_cost_entries`) still records their cost.
4. **What stays.** The per-run US$7 and per-day US$25 backstops, which the owner reconfirmed with the cap removed (OD-2026-10-25-2); credit holds and reservations, and the vendor reservation that books every attempt; the run breaker (three consecutive failed products); the member ceiling; ADR-0027's turn limits, `cost_budget` and `cap_below_turn`; the Anthropic Console monthly limit; every authority rule.
5. **The verify rules** (`scripts/provider-checks.mjs`, run by `npm run verify`):
   - Per-product versions: "the default selection must exist and cost one credit" becomes "the default selection must be an entry of this version"; "the default member ceiling must admit the default selection" stays, and the ceiling must also equal the default's credits. "Every selection's worst case per product is at most 90% of its credits' net revenue" stays. "The unrecovered-cost cap is at most the rest" applies to version 1 only, which keeps its published, pinned US$4.50 that nothing enforces; any other version that carries an unrecovered-cost cap is refused.
   - Loop versions: the per-run and per-day caps stay required (the daily cap at least the run cap, two worst-case turns within the run backstop); an unrecovered-cost cap is no longer required, and a loop version that carries one is refused. A loop version must carry item 7's look-for-work cap (`look_for_work_daily_cost_cap_usd`), at most the run backstop and enough for two worst-case turns of its installed default.
6. **Catalogue version 2 stays `proposed`.** This decision changes only its installed default, removes its unrecovered-cost cap and adds item 7's look-for-work cap. Its prices, `max_tokens`, turn and tool limits still await the owner's confirmation with its publication (Q-catalogue-v2-values, ADR-0027 item 11).
7. **The look-for-work cap: US$1.12 per agent per day** (OD-2026-10-25-2; amends ADR-0026 item 4). The daily look-for-work pass (ADR-0024 item 6: read-only, one per agent a day) keeps the installed default, Opus 5.5 at `medium`. ADR-0026 item 4's cap of 1% of the monthly grant per pass (about US$0.61 at US$149 and US$0.41 at US$99) is replaced by a per-agent daily cap of US$1.12 of worst-case vendor cost: two worst-case turns at that default, a tool turn and a final turn, whatever the plan price.
   - A pass holds the lowest of its turn limit times its worst-case turn, US$7 and US$1.12. At the default it holds US$1.12 and covers two worst-case turns. A profile whose two worst-case turns exceed US$1.12, such as Opus 5.5 at `high` (US$1.28), is not admitted for the pass (`cap_below_turn`); a cheaper one fits more turns within the same US$1.12. A second pass the same day is refused (`trigger_used`: one run per trigger key).
   - Where it lives: loop catalogue version 2's `look_for_work_daily_cost_cap_usd` (`"1.12"`, generated as `lookForWorkDailyCostCapUsd12` in 10^-12 USD, the unit of the run and daily backstops beside it); `lookForWorkHold` and `lookForWorkDailyCapUsd12` in `@morrow/policy/loop-catalogue`; and RateCard@1's `caps.lookForWorkDailyUsd`, a USD amount in place of `lookForWorkPassBasisPoints` (no rate card is published; merchants may still lower the cap, and only the Owner raises it).
   - No code admits a pass yet. Build order PR 21's `admit_mandate_run` (ADR-0024 item 6) takes this value and rule, and its labs test them.

## Security invariants touched

- **Budget checks in code:** the per-run and per-day backstops and the breaker still refuse (`tests/model-defaults-cost-cap.test.mjs`, `lab:run-credits`, `lab:listings-runs`); `providers.test.mjs` refuses a cap on any version but 1.
- **Budget checks in code (item 7):** one look-for-work pass at the installed default is admitted, and a second the same day or a pass at Opus 5.5 `high` is refused (`loop-catalogue.test.mjs`, `tests/model-defaults-cost-cap.test.mjs`); verify refuses a loop version whose look-for-work cap is missing, above the run backstop or below two worst-case turns of its installed default (`loop-catalogue.test.mjs`); RateCard@1 refuses a look-for-work cap in basis points or another currency (`agent-platform-contracts.test.mjs`).
- **Profiles are never authority:** a default changes no tool, capability, grant or cap (`loop-catalogue.test.mjs`, `tool-catalogue.test.mjs`).
- **Immutable migrations:** 0051 is a forward migration; 0033's helper is replaced only from its pinned body (`migration-history.test.mjs`).

## Consequences

- **Cost per run rises.** An installed agent's worst-case turn is US$0.56 at Opus 5.5 `medium` (US$0.21 at Sonnet 5.5 `low`), its turn limit 12 and its hold US$6.72 (US$3.36); an expected six-turn run is about US$0.56 (US$0.21). A Listings product at the default costs 4 credits (1), about US$0.062 of model use expected (US$0.018) and US$0.544 at worst (US$0.132), and a run at the default drafts at most 12 products (25).
- **The ceilings.** The per-run US$7 and per-day US$25 backstops and the Anthropic Console monthly limit are now the only limits on AI cost that earns no credit: up to US$25 a workspace a day, more than a month's plan price within a week. The breaker still stops a run after three failed products.
- **Look-for-work passes.** At the installed default a pass holds US$1.12 and is expected to cost about US$0.23 (a first turn of US$0.14 and a second of US$0.084). At worst one agent's passes cost US$1.12 a day, about US$34 in a 30-day month, and the five installed agents' about US$168, which the per-workspace US$25 daily backstop also counts. Under the 1% cap no Opus 5.5 pass fitted at either price.
- **The grant rule** (ADR-0026 item 5) is measured on staging at these defaults; if the default load passes 50% of the grant, billing proposes lower defaults for the owner's confirmation.
- **Deployment.** The Workers' generated catalogue and the database name the same active version, so 0051 and the code that names version 3 deploy together; between the two, Listings admission is refused `catalogue_inactive`, never admitted at the wrong version.

## Approval

The owner's statement in chat, verbatim, on 2026-10-25, answering the lead's note on the agent loop's defaults and on the unrecovered-cost cap blocking every Opus run before billing (OD-2026-10-25-1):

> use opus 5.5 high for the main agent and opus 5.5 medium for all type of subagents/specialists etc. and you can remove the cap on ai cost

What it approves: items 1 and 3, and item 2's default and removed cap. The lead's reading, confirmed to the owner in chat: "all type of subagents/specialists" covers every agent kind but the Coordinator, the installed agents and Listings included; the backstops, holds, breaker, monthly limit and authority rules stay; catalogue version 2's other values are not confirmed by this answer.

The owner's answers in chat, verbatim, the same day, to three questions the lead put after that answer, each quoted with the option the owner chose (OD-2026-10-25-2):

> Q1: "With Listings on Opus 5.5 medium, each product it drafts costs 4 credits instead of 1 (your 300 test credits = about 75 products). Who in a shop may run it at that default?" Answer: "Everyone, up to 4 (Recommended)"

> Q2: "Each agent's daily 'look for useful work' pass (read-only, proposes nothing) has a cost cap of about 1% of the plan price. One Opus 5.5 medium pass costs more than that cap, so these passes would never run. What should they use?" Answer: "Opus medium, raise the cap" (the option said: keep Opus 5.5 medium even for the daily check and raise its cap to fit, about US$1.12 per agent per day worst case)

> Q3: "With the unbilled-AI-cost cap removed, the remaining safety limits are US$7 per run and US$25 per workspace per day, plus your Anthropic monthly limit. Keep those two?" Answer: "Keep US$7 / US$25 (Recommended)"

What they approve: Q1, item 2's default member ceiling of 4; Q2, item 7, the look-for-work pass at the installed default under a cap of US$1.12 per agent per day; Q3, item 4's per-run US$7 and per-day US$25 backstops, kept unchanged.

Written after the answers, to carry them out: item 5's rule changes, and where item 7's value lives in data and code. Item 2's ceiling follows ADR-0022 item 16's rule that the default ceiling admits the default; it replaces the 1 the owner confirmed on 2026-10-19 (value 7). Item 7's US$1.12 is exactly two worst-case turns of Opus 5.5 at `medium` (US$0.56 each).

What the ceiling widens: in a workspace with no stored ceiling (admission and `GET /models` fall back to the version's default ceiling), a Member without `governance.model_default.revise` may pick any entry of at most 4 credits a product (Opus 5.5 at `low` or `medium`, Sonnet 5.5 at `medium` or `high`), up to 48 credits and US$6.528 of worst-case vendor cost a run at Opus 5.5 `medium` (12 products) or 57 credits at Sonnet 5.5 `high` (19 products); under the ceiling of 1 that Member was held to 1-credit entries, at most 25 credits and US$3.30 a run. Version 3's `owner_confirmation` quotes Q1's answer, and Q-adr-0036-member-ceiling is answered.
