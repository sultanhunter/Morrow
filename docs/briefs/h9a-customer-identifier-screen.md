# H9a: The customer identifier screen

- Status: brief, 2026-10-26. Branch `claude/h9a-customer-identifier-screen`. Plan row H9a of `docs/plan/harness-foundations-implementation.md` (size M, after H6; parallel with H5, H6, H12a and H14). Change class: material (customer data and retention), already owner-approved: ADR-0041 item 31 and ADR-0040 item 2, owner question 23 of OD-2026-10-26-7 ("customer details typed into messages are refused until P09.T05; order numbers pass"). No new decision is taken here. No migration: the screen is code, the refusal is a closed contract, and no table changes.

## Goal

Until P09.T05's deletion and privacy path exists, Morrow keeps no customer details (CLAUDE.md). This PR builds the one screen in code, outside any prompt, that keeps them out of what Morrow stores: a person's post (ADR-0040's post command, H11b) and every text a model writes that Morrow commits (ADR-0041 items 13 and 31). It wires the screen into the three model-written write paths that exist on `main` today, gives the refusal its closed contract and the records' exact words, and puts the standing line under every composer that writes to an agent.

## Scope

- **The screen** (`packages/policy/src/customer-screen.ts`, exported as `@morrow/policy/customer-screen`; pure, synchronous apart from one port, no I/O, no `node:` import, so it runs in the api, runtime and broker Workers). It reads a scan copy that is never stored: NFKC-folded, format characters (zero-width, bidi, soft hyphen, variation selectors, fillers) dropped, HTML references and percent escapes of the characters an address or id needs (with `%25`, so a double-encoded `%2540` is read) decoded up to three times, every script's decimal digits made ASCII, every dash "-", bracketed "(at)" and "(dot)" closed up; the email, customer id and address scans read it with every run of spaces and tabs closed up to one space, and the phone scan reads it with its runs kept. It finds, by kind (`CustomerDataKind@v1.1`):
  - `email`: an address (not an image or file name whose at sign precedes a scale, such as a 2x logo), also obfuscated; a spaced ("jane @ shop.com") or written-out ("jane.doe at shop dot com") form when its local part is address-like (a dot, underscore or plus, or letters with digits) or its domain is a personal mail provider; and `jane@gmail` without its top-level domain;
  - `phone`: a run of digits classified by its shape and the words before it (a phone word makes 7 to 15 digits a phone; an id or money word makes it not one; otherwise an international number, a grouped one, or a bare national one with its trunk 0 or a valid North American number); a second pass reads a run glued to "=", ",", "/" or "." (key=value and CSV forms; not after a digit, except a strongly phone-shaped run after a digit and a comma, a CSV row's phone column after a numeric one) and, after a phone word, to "#" or "-" ("Tel#…"); a run that merges a phone with a number before or after it by spaces or tabs is read again in parts, counting only a strongly phone-shaped part (led by "+", a trunk 0 in one token or in national groups, "00" in one token, or a bracketed North American number); and `tel:`, `wa.me` and WhatsApp's send links;
  - `street_address`: shapes, never a gazetteer (a label glued with ":" or "=" and a house number glued to its street name with "-" are split off first): a house number with up to four name words and a street word (words that are also ordinary nouns, such as Drive, Court, Place and Way, only in title case and never after the words that make them ordinary), a number, street type and name ("10 rue de Rivoli"), a street type, name and number ("Calle Mayor 5"), compound streets ("Hauptstraße 12"), unit and street pairs ("House 12, Street 5"), PO boxes, and postcodes or ZIPs in their place (UK, Canadian, "IL 62704" after a comma, "zip 90210");
  - `customer_gid`: a Customer, CustomerAddress, MailingAddress, CustomerPaymentMethod, CustomerSegmentMember or CompanyContact GID, plain, percent-encoded or base64; a customer link in the Shopify admin or the Admin API; `customer:<id>`; a numeric customer id of seven digits or more after the word "customer" (`customer_id`, `customerId`, "Customer ID:");
  - `listed_email`: a looser email form (a plain-word local part, spaced or written out) whose keyed digest is on the customer email list.
  Links, GIDs, addresses, Morrow record ids (`product:N` ids and UUIDs), ISO dates, times and IPv4 addresses are masked before the phone and address scans, so order GIDs, order numbers (`#1042`), product and variant ids, SKUs, tracking and barcode numbers, prices, counts, dates and times pass. What a link says is still read: each link's path, query and fragment, decoded ("+" a space unless it starts a value, percent escapes read, each "/", "?", "&" and "#" a line break), is masked and scanned for phones and addresses on its own, so a map link's street or a `?phone=` is found while an order link's ids read as ids. A link is `https://`, `www.` or a bare host with its top-level domain followed by "/" or "?" (`maps.google.com/?q=…`); an email address's domain is not one. Inside a link, a bare number shaped like a North American phone is an id (a Sheets tab's `#gid=`, a legacy admin id) unless a phone word or key comes first or it is the link's whole content (`jane.doe/4155552671`); a "+", "00" or trunk 0 still reads as a phone. Every pattern is bounded and linear; a text is at most 1 MiB, raw and once folded and decoded, and a call at most 4 MiB (counted the same way) and 4,096 strings (a longer one is a caller defect that throws). A link's content is decoded on its own and can be up to about twice the link (a percent-encoded U+FDFA, nine characters, folds to eighteen there), so the links of a mebibyte are read as at most about two; the linear-time test times that shape.
- **The verdict and the refusal.** The verdict names kinds only, sorted in the contract's order and frozen, never the text or what matched. `CustomerScreenRefusal@1` (`{schemaVersion 1, code customer_data, kinds}`, v1.1 core, release baseline updated) is the same for a post and for a model-written text. `ToolRefusalCode`'s `customer_data` now also covers a text the model wrote; plan.draft's refusal union gains `customer_data`.
- **The keyed customer email list** (`CustomerEmailDigestList`). ADR-0041 item 31 calls it "the keyed email-digest list that `customers/redact` already uses"; precisely, `customers/redact` today keeps a keyed digest of the customer **id** only (`apps/broker/src/shopify.ts`, `morrow.shopify.customer.v1|<id>` under the app secret), and no customer email is kept anywhere. The screen therefore holds the list as a port: it hands the broker the digest inputs of the looser forms (`customerEmailDigestInput`: NFKC, lower case, `+tag` dropped, `morrow.customer-email.v1|` prefix) and the broker answers one boolean; it is asked only when no pattern matched and a looser form exists; "unavailable", a throw or any non-boolean answer fails closed as `screen_unavailable` (nothing stored; retryable); more than 32 looser forms in one call are refused as `email` without asking. Until P09.T05 the list is empty (`noListedCustomerEmails`), so the synchronous screen is complete; P09.T05 binds the broker's list and moves each caller to the listed entry point.
- **Where it runs.**
  - The post command (ADR-0040 item 2; built in H11b): `screenPost(texts, list)` is its first check inside the command's transaction, before the message, inbox row or any run sees the text. A match answers `delivery not_started`, `code customer_data`, the refusal and the safe message, and stores nothing.
  - Every model-written stored text: `modelWrittenTexts` lists each kind ADR-0041 names (conversation summaries, the fallback checkpoint, compaction summaries, run plans and their notes, plan cards, `understood`, ledger titles, result items, findings, teammate results, agent messages, notebook entries, knowledge proposals, memory suggestions), with the decision that screens it today; a flagged compaction summary is `fallback_reset` (dropped for the reset on code facts), every other kind `refuse`. Wired here, in the decision before the command, refusal `customer_data`, nothing sent to SQL: `decideKnowledgePropose` (title, text and source values), `decideNotebookWrite` (text), `decidePlanDraft` (every string and key of the mapped card). The other kinds' writers have not landed; the PR that first writes each one calls `screenModelWritten` in the same step and records its decision in the registry (H5, H7b, H7d, H7e, H10, H11a, H11b, H12b, H12c).
- **The copy, exactly as the records give it.** The safe message is "Morrow can't keep customer details yet. Remove them and send again." (ADR-0040 item 2, ADR-0041 item 31). The standing line "Don't include customers' names or contact details." sits under every composer that writes to an agent: the project conversation, the private Ask and its free answer, a new project's request to Morrow, an answer to an agent's question in the Inbox, and the Listings request. `TextArea` and `TextField` gain a `standing` line read with the field (`aria-describedby`). The post route's safe message reaches the person through `CommandFeedback`'s `invalid` message when H11b lands; no UI mapping is needed.
- **What it cannot catch: customer names.** A name typed as free text ("Jane Smith asked for a refund") matches no pattern, and before P09.T05 Morrow holds no list of names to compare with. Such a name would reach messages, run turns, summaries and memory, where `customers/redact` cannot find it. The two controls that narrow it are the standing line (this PR) and message redaction that deletes every copy (ADR-0041 item 14). The owner chose option (a) on that question, recorded as OD-2026-10-26-10 (pull request 86): P09.T05's deletion path comes first, built by SD4 of the store-data foundation plan, and ADR-0040's `async-conversations` switch stays off until it exists and passes its tests (the H20 row's gate). How a typed name is matched, and its `lab:shopify-privacy` case, belong to row H9d of the harness plan, a P09.T05 child, not to this PR. So this PR builds no redaction and no name matching: the tables that hold a message's copies (`messages`, `run_turns`, epochs, checkpoints, derived memory) do not exist yet, and H9d registers them `content: customer` on SD4's base.
- **What it also refuses: the shop's own contact details.** No pattern can tell the shop's support address, phone or street from a customer's; owner question 23 approved the screen for every email, phone and street address. A post or a knowledge proposal naming the shop's own support address is refused until P09.T05 (open question 2).
- **Not screened here:** Listings drafts (`run_drafts`) are product copy from a run that reads only the catalogue, never customer data, and the review screen already counts the URLs, emails and phones a draft adds; person-typed fields outside a post (task objectives, question answers, knowledge pages a person writes, plan edits) are not in item 31's list (open question 1).

## Security notes

- Customer data: the screen is the code half of CLAUDE.md's rule that customer data never enters before P09.T05; the read-tag rule (a run that read customer data proposes and notes nothing) is unchanged and runs first. Nothing of a refused text is stored, logged or returned: the refusal names kinds only, and the lab searches every table for the planted values.
- Fail closed: an unanswerable keyed list is `screen_unavailable`; an input outside the bounds throws; a structured value that is not plain JSON throws.
- Authority and tenancy: unchanged. The screen grants nothing and reads no tenant; it runs in code before the definer, so no SQL changes and no migration.
- Model untrusted: the screen runs on what the model wrote, keys included, never on what the model says about it; obfuscations a model could produce (escapes, full-width and zero-width characters, other scripts' digits, base64 and percent-encoded GIDs) are decoded first.
- Denial of service: every pattern is linear: each whitespace and repeated-piece quantifier is bounded, a token is trimmed without a trailing-run pattern, and a run of spaces is closed up before the email and address scans. The bounds hold on the scan copy, not only the raw text: NFKC makes U+FDFA eighteen characters and a decoded reference can expand again, so the fold and each decoding pass are checked as they are made and an overlong one throws `CUSTOMER_SCREEN_TEXT_TOO_LONG` before any scan reads it (H9A-2R4). A mebibyte of adversarial text takes at most 1.9 seconds of CPU time (a mebibyte of links holding percent-encoded U+FDFA, whose contents fold to about twice the text; the next shape 1.3 seconds; 2026-10-27); in wall time that was one to ten seconds on the Mac at load 250 to 750 and up to 26 seconds at load near 1,000. The unit test times 48 shapes (long runs of spaces, tabs and punctuation, masked spans, links, bare hosts, merged digit runs, and shapes that expand when folded or decoded, in the scan copy or in a link's content) at a 64th, a 16th, a quarter and all of the bound, and two shapes whose fold is exactly at the bound, at most 20 seconds of the process's CPU time each (CPU, not wall time, since 2026-10-27: at load near 1,000 the slowest shape's 1.9 s of CPU took 24 s of wall time, while the module before H9A-R1 still grows fourfold per doubling in CPU time and fails at a quarter of the bound), so a quadratic pattern fails within minutes instead of hanging the run.
