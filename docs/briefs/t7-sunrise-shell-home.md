## Goal

The workspace looks like Sunrise: the Shopify admin made a little warmer, agents as friendly characters, plain words. The shell and sidebar follow the boards, and Home answers "what needs me today" from the data that exists now.

## Scope

1. **Tokens.** `docs/design/tokens.json` moves to schema 2 with the Sunrise values: colours with their contrast pairs (61, all passing), Inter, the type scale, a 4 px spacing scale, radii, the six shadows, 44/48 px target floors and the 1280 px panel breakpoint. `scripts/generate-ui-tokens.mjs` validates every section as a closed shape and generates `tokens.css` and `tokens.ts`. Option 1's colour names stay as aliases until no stylesheet uses them, so parallel work in the old look still renders.
2. **Styles.** `base`, `shell`, `components`, `avatars` and `screens` stylesheets rewritten in Sunrise; every control is at least 44 px (48 below 760 px).
3. **Agent characters.** `components/avatars.tsx`: Morrow, Listings, Business Analyst, Inventory, Support, Marketing and a specialist with five props, the "Agent" and "Specialist · one-time" pills, people's initials with presence, and the store's monogram tile.
4. **Shell.** The store switcher, the Ask Morrow button on every page, Search (drawn, disabled), then the route contract's sidebar in its order: Home, Projects, Inbox with its count, Team (Morrow always listed), Files, Shop knowledge, Settings at the bottom, and the store connection line. Below 760 px: a 56 px top bar and the existing modal drawer. Labels only, four of them: "Work" shows as Projects, "Agents" as Team, "Assets" as Files, "Knowledge" as Shop knowledge. Route ids, paths, the order, the sidebar's link targets (`people_and_agents`, the display-only credit balance on Settings > Usage), the route contract and its titles don't change; tab titles use the Sunrise names. The Inbox count and connection line read again on each page change, command change and Retry, under the shell's refresh, which Home's reads share (a Retry on Home reads the sidebar's copy too); a re-read with no answer keeps the last ok reply. The pages that hold a command (the connection screens and Ask) read connections once per mount, so no background re-read can unmount a command's unknown outcome and its Check again. **Settings frame** (the ConnectStore board): the settings and store connection screens sit beside a sub-navigation card (Plan and credits, People and roles, Notifications, Model default, Shopify connection), and Activity is listed there, not in the sidebar, as a placeholder until it has a route.
5. **Home.** The date line, greeting, one primary ("Review N items" or "Ask Morrow"; "Nothing needs you" and the Ask Morrow primary only when the open-questions read answered, never on a denied or failed read), scope line and waiting chips; the cards Needs you (open questions), Recent work (goals) and Your store (connection); the ADR-0015 pre-payment notice as the one banner.
6. **Home preview switch.** The board's sections without a backend (Today, Your projects, Yesterday at a glance, Ideas, the Ask Morrow panel, pinned projects, the Team list, credits) render only from a new port method `samples.home`. The HTTP build never composes it (not_in_build, nothing sent); the fixture composes it only in the `home_preview` scenario. Every sample card says "Sample data", and so do the sidebar's sample rows (a visible tag, ", sample data" to a screen reader).
7. **Entry screens.** Sign-in, workspace setup and the account screens get the SignIn board's top bar (Morrow's character over a rule) and one centred white card, 504 px at most, with full-width actions; sign-in and signed out open with Morrow on the horizon. The store connection screens restyle through the shared styles and the Settings frame only; their page is named "Shopify connection".
8. **Docs.** `docs/design/sunrise.md` (the specification the code depends on); notes in the component baseline, prototype guide and Mobbin guide.

## Security notes

- **The screens decide nothing.** Every action still goes through a screen whose command the server checks; Home and the sidebar only read (open questions, goals, connections) and link.
- **Default deny for the preview.** `samples.home` has no route and no contract; `http/composed.ts` keeps it uncomposed, so the production build answers not_in_build without a request (a unit test asserts zero requests). Sample data lives only under `apps/workspace-web/src/fixtures`, which the production entry can't import (`verify:workspace-web`).
- **No new origin.** No font, image or script from another host: the CSP is unchanged, and Inter falls back to the system font until a self-hosted file lands.
- **Pictures confer nothing.** A character, colour, pill or title grants no authority (CLAUDE.md, ADR-0032 item 3). People never show as agents and agents never show presence.
- **No colour literal** outside `tokens.css` (`verify:design`), and no violet in the tokens (unit test).
