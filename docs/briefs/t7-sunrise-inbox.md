## Goal

One Inbox for what only a person can settle: changes to review, questions, problems Morrow can't settle alone, and a store that isn't connected. Home, the sidebar and the Inbox count the same read. Settings > Activity shows what happened, in plain words, over the audit records that exist.

## Scope

1. **`GET /workspace/:ws/work/inbox`** (`packages/work/src/inbox/{http,read,sql}.ts`; a dispatch row ahead of the work API): one READ ONLY transaction aggregating, per viewer and filtered on the server by each record's own rule: open questions addressed to the viewer (`question.read` view, as `GET /questions?respondent=me`; a denial marks the section `denied`, never "nothing waits"); pending approvals the viewer may decide (the approvals library's reviewer role), each with whether its store is linked now (a change for a disconnected store can't be applied, so it isn't counted); apply outcomes that are unknown (`uncertain`, open) or not applied in the last seven days (`failed`, `conflict`; information, never counted, as InboxItem's `failed_operation`), visible as the operation read is (the proposal's reviewer or proposer); and connection problems (an open stop on a store connection whose store isn't linked again: a person's disconnect, an uninstall, a scope change), only for a viewer holding the store's disconnect right. Item kinds follow the contract's InboxKind (`question`, `approval`, `exception` with `uncertain_operation`/`failed_operation`) plus `connection`, the internal SPA view of ADR-0025 item 10's Reconnect; this is the SPA's internal JSON, not InboxItem@1.
2. **`GET /workspace/:ws/work/activity`** (`inbox/activity.ts`): one keyset page (newest first, at most 100 records scanned, at most 50 shown) over approval decisions, the gateway's operation transitions, KillSwitchRecords (Stop AI work, Disconnect store), system connection stops, agent installations and Listings runs, each entry shown only to a person who may see its record (the proposal's reviewer or proposer; the stop's actor or a holder of the same stop right; the store's steward; the run's task and product read). Ids, closed codes, counts and times only: never a person's stop reason or review comment, a product id or content.
3. **Screens.** The Inbox (`screens/inbox.tsx`; the Inbox, EmptyStates, Phone and MemberHome boards): filter chips (All, To review, Questions, Problems) with open counts, the count note, the rows (each opening its own screen: the review screen, the question, the exception screen, Shopify connection), the one banner for a disconnected store, empty, filter-empty, loading, couldn't load, offline and denied-section states, and the preview of not-yet-built kinds (`samples.inbox`, default off, "Sample data"). The exception screen (`screens/exception.tsx`, the route contract's existing `exception` route, until now unbuilt): the result in plain words with the review screen's own Check again and "I checked it in Shopify". Home (Needs you, the header and chips) and the sidebar and top bar count from the same read (`useInbox`, under the shell's refresh); the open-questions read they used before (`useOpenQuestions`, `waitingCount`, `byUrgency`) is retired. Settings > Activity (`screens/activity.tsx`) keeps Stop all AI work as it is (person-only, typed confirmation) and lists the log, by day, with the board's filters and Show earlier.
4. **Port and adapters.** A port group `inbox.read`, `inbox.operation` (the review routes' existing `GET /work/operations/:id`) and `inbox.activity`, composed over HTTP with closed decoders (`http/inbox-decoders.ts`); `samples.inbox` uncomposed over HTTP; PR 16's fixture-only `settings.activity` is replaced by `inbox.activity`. Fixture scenarios `inbox_problems`, `inbox_preview` and `activity_more`.

## Security notes

- **The server decides every item.** Each Inbox item and activity entry passes the same check as its own screen, in code, on the server; the browser only counts and links what it was given. A section the viewer may not read is `denied`, so Home never shows a false all-clear.
- **Tenancy unchanged.** Both reads run as `morrow_work_api` under the existing FORCE RLS `tenant_read` policies and column grants: no migration, no new grant. Another tenant's session is 401; another workspace's records are invisible by RLS.
- **No content, no secrets.** The statements select ids, codes and times only (a unit test pins that no statement selects a stop reason, comment, canonical, snapshot or content); a question's prompt is the one text and renders as text (a unit test renders markup in a prompt escaped; no screen uses `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`).
- **Unknown outcomes stay unknown.** An `uncertain` operation is "Needs attention" with the three dots, never done; the exception screen only offers the review screen's existing read-back and set-aside commands, which the server checks. Nothing here sends a change.
- **Default off.** `samples.inbox` has no route and is never composed over HTTP (a unit test asserts no request); fixtures live only under `apps/workspace-web/src/fixtures`.
- **Stops unchanged.** Stop all AI work is PR 16's (person-only, typed confirmation, each store's own stop); the log records stops and disconnects for those who govern them.

## Review findings (AI review, separate context, same model family) and fixes

- **F2 (medium) `more` came before the per-viewer filter.** It was set from the 51st operation row and from the approvals page's cursor, neither checked against what the viewer may see; a person who could see none of the items got `more: true` with no items, so Home said "Nothing needs you" with Ask Morrow and the Inbox showed "0+" beside "You're all caught up", and the flag told them hidden records exist. Fixed: `inboxChangeItems` keeps the first 50 operations the viewer may see from the first 100 rows (the statement now sends 101, one to tell the bound from the end) and sets `more` only for one more visible unknown outcome past them; `inboxApprovals` reads the approvals list page after page (at most four) until 25 the viewer may decide and sets `more` only for a 26th. Hidden records never set it; a scan that ends at its bound undecided sets it only beside a full section, never beside an empty one. The SPA treats `more` as not clear: Home's header offers no all-clear and no Ask Morrow primary, its Needs you card links to the Inbox instead of "Nothing needs you", and the Inbox shows "You're all caught up" only when nothing more waits. Tests: 51 and 101 hidden unknown outcomes give `more: false` (the old test asserted `true`); the two scans with fakes; `homeSummary` with `more` and no open item; the Inbox list and Needs you rendered with `more`.
- **F3 (medium) the copy promised a check Morrow doesn't make.** "It checks the product again on its own", "It keeps checking the product on its own" and "Morrow keeps checking it" are untrue: a change Morrow couldn't confirm is read back right after the apply and when a person presses Check again; nothing reconciles it on a schedule. Fixed: the words say what happens ("Use Check again to read the product now", "Check again reads the product", "Nothing was sent again"). Test: no Inbox copy says "on its own", "keeps checking", "automatically" and the like.
- **F1 (low) the exception screen promised a read-back for a change that wasn't applied.** It always showed the read-back line, so a `failed` or `conflict` change with no read-back read "Morrow hasn't checked the product yet." (the fixture's `inbox/exception_failed`). No read-back ever comes: Check again is offered only for `uncertain` and `cancel_requested` (review.tsx), and 0045's reconcile reads back only submitted, uncertain and cancel_requested operations; a conflict found before sending and a definite failure are never read back. The same class of claim F3 retired. Fixed: `checksLine` shows the line only for a change Morrow couldn't confirm or a person set aside, or for any operation with a read-back (then counted, with the last check's day); otherwise the screen leaves it out. With none so far the words are "Morrow hasn't checked the product." (no "yet"). Tests: the F3 claims also refuse "hasn't checked ... yet" and check the counts 0-2 directly (the copy walk never passes 0); the fixture's conflict operation …0202 renders no read-back line, nor do `failed` and `verified` with none; `uncertain` and `cancel_requested` with none say so plainly; a read-back is counted whatever the status; `ui:check`'s `inbox/exception_failed` asserts no such line. Each new assertion fails on the unfixed copy or screen.
- **F4 (low) the exception screen undercounted read-backs.** It counted the read-backs the server sends, which are the 20 latest. Fixed: it counts from the newest read-back's sequence, which numbers the operation's read-backs from 1 (0045's two inserts take the operation's max+1, and the table is append-only); the decoder now refuses read-backs out of order. Test: 20 read-backs ending at sequence 37 read "37 times"; the statement's order and limit and 0045's numbering are pinned.

## Open questions

1. Activity stays on `settings_section` `governance` (PR 16's mapping). Giving it an `activity` section is a route-contract change; recommended as one small contract PR with the Email addresses section.
2. Connection problems show only to the store's disconnect-right holders (ADR-0025 item 10's steward and Owner); other members see the sidebar's connection line. Confirm that members without the right should not get an Inbox item.
3. Not-applied changes stay listed (not counted) for seven days; there is no "dismiss" yet (no read state is stored). Confirm the window, or ask for dismiss with stored read state (InboxItem@1's `readState`, T6).
4. Seam with PR 5 (store connection health). A `connection` item is an open connection stop on a store that is not linked now, so a person's disconnect, an uninstall and today's scope-change stop (which suspends the installation) show. Under ADR-0025 item 10 a scope change keeps the store linked; its system stop is then not a `connection` item here, and PR 5's Reconnect item (the shape `samples.inbox` previews) is expected to carry it. PR 5 should add Reconnect as its own item kind in this read rather than widen `connection`.
5. People in the activity log show as "Person · 1a2b" (the repository's `personLabel`) until `people.list` exists (§4.14); the board's names need that read.
6. The log covers the store's records (decisions, the gateway's outcomes, stops, connection ends, agents added, Listings requests). Goal, task and question commands (`morrow_work.commands`) and an Admin's read of someone's conversation (the board's footnote) are not listed yet; each needs its own visibility rule. Until they are, the board's intro ("Everything people, agents and Morrow did", PR 16's copy) says more than the log holds; keep it, or say "What happened at your store" until then.
7. Both reads decide a proposal's visibility by reading its row once per distinct proposal (as the approvals list does): on a busy log, up to about 100 sequential statements inside the 5-second read. After F2 the Inbox may also read up to 100 operations' proposals and four pages of the approvals list when much is hidden from the viewer. If hosted latency makes that tight, one batched proposal read per page is the follow-up. Past those bounds, a viewer for whom more than 50 of the first 100 unknown outcomes (or four pages of approvals) are hidden may not learn that a visible one waits further on; the alternative, `more` beside an empty Inbox, is what F2 retired.
8. The two reads have no switch: they read records that exist, as PR 3's review reads do, and fail closed (one malformed record is a 503 for the whole Inbox, never a partial one). Say if the lead wants a default-off switch on them instead.
9. The review screen's own copy (PR 3's, on `main`: `copy.review.setAsideHint` "Nothing is sent again, and Morrow keeps checking." and `setAsideNote` "…Morrow still checks it.", and `port.ts`'s comment) makes the claim F3 retired here, and the exception screen shows it through the review screen's operation list. Recommended: the same wording fix in PR 4, which changes the review screen, or a background reconciler (PR 3 follow-up) that makes the claim true.
