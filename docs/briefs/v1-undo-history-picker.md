## Goal

The owner can take back a change Morrow applied, see every change on a task with its result, approve the changes waiting on a task with one confirmation, and pick products on Ask instead of typing numbers. Every one of them keeps the gateway's rules: an exact approval per change, a recheck before each write, nothing written when the product changed.

## Scope

- **Undo** (`POST /work/operations/:id/undo`, empty body, an Idempotency-Key). Offered on a change Shopify confirmed (`verified`), for 90 days from its send while the values read before it are held (the draft's snapshot follows ADR-0031's product-data clock and shop/redact deletes it). The request (1) checks the person may read the change's proposal and holds `view` on `catalog.products.read`, `propose` and `delegate` on `catalog.content.publish` with the change's fields on the store resource; (2) asks the broker to read the product once (`ApiBroker.shopifyProductUndoCheck`: never a write) and compare each listing field with what the apply left; (3) when every field is unchanged, stores a NEW `catalog.content.publish` proposal that sets each field the change wrote back to its earlier value, on the product as Shopify holds it now (its `expectedSourceRevision` is that read), proposed by the store's Listings installation for the person (`cmd_catalog_undo` rebuilds and rechecks every part in SQL). The person reviews and approves it like any other change (its own digest and approval) and applies it through the same gateway. A product changed since the apply stops Undo and names the fields (nothing written); a change between the undo and its apply is refused by the gateway's pre-send read. Undo asked again within 10 seconds of a recorded check (a lost reply, or a second person) reads nothing more: it builds on that check, or stops on it the same way. A second undo while one waits, or after one applied, is refused. After 90 days or shop/redact it says "no longer available".
- **History** (`GET /work/tasks/:taskId/operations` gains `undo`, one entry per change in the same order): `none` (not confirmed in Shopify: an unknown outcome keeps its badge until read back and is never undone), `available` (until when), `requested` or `undone` (the undo's proposal), `changed` (Morrow wrote the product again since) or `unavailable`; a change that was itself an undo says so. The review document gains `undoOf` (the change an undo proposal undoes). The task's "Changes applied" tab shows each product's badge, then its Undo line.
- **"Approve several at once"** (`GET /work/proposals/:id/batch`, `POST …/batch-decision`): the server builds the document of the pending root proposals of the same task, capability and store that this person may approve (2 to 10), with each one's exact digest, approval and revision, bound to the person and session, 5 minutes, single use. A solo owner types one phrase for the whole batch ("approve N changes for C credits", summed: ADR-0024 item 9). The decision rebuilds the document at its own time and must reach the same digest, checks the phrase, then decides each member through the ordinary decide path (reviewer, digest, revision, policy, counted reviewers, each member's own phrase) in one transaction: one refusal refuses them all. Each approved change is still applied on its own and rechecked by the gateway. The review screen shows the button and its confirmation (the Inbox builder does not touch the review screen).
- **Product picker on Ask**: search the chosen store's products (letters and digits, up to five words) or show a collection's, page by page, and tick products into the list. Reads go through M2 PR-3's catalogue routes (the session, the store's current link to this workspace, `view` on `catalog.products.read`, a read lease, the broker's read): ids, titles and status only; titles render as text.

Non-goals: undo of a change Shopify never confirmed, of fields beyond the five listing fields, or of a value longer than Morrow can write (it says so); a batch over several tasks or more than 10 proposals; the Inbox's own batch entry (PR 15).

## Security notes

- **No shortcut.** An undo is a new proposal with its own digest and pending approval; nothing about the original approval carries over, and the gateway applies it exactly as any other (claim, lease, stops, link, installation, `write_products`, entitlement, the pre-send read against the undo's `expectedSourceRevision`, the fence, one send, the read-back). `cmd_catalog_undo` accepts only the one target and item it rebuilds itself from records (the operation's product, store, connection and fields; the undo check's digest; each field set to the draft snapshot's value through 0038's `draft_set_valid`), only from the store's active Listings installation, under the workspace's current policy and pending cap, and only while the change's undo state is `available` under the operation's row lock and the store's stop key (shared; shop/redact holds it exclusively, so its pass either empties these parameters or ran first).
- **Fail-safe comparison.** The undo check calls a field unchanged only when the read-back's canonical comparison proves it equals what the apply left (the draft's snapshot with the approved set applied); anything else is `changed` and Undo stops. The check must be the change's latest, at most 10 minutes old (one the request did not record itself, at most 30 seconds), and its read is stored as a snapshot.
- **Custody and records.** The broker's undo definers (`catalog_undo_resolve`, `catalog_undo_record`) run only as `morrow_vault_broker` (`work_executor_safe`), keyed by one operation id, reaching exactly the operation and product their own transaction-local settings name; the token is opened only in the broker; only codes cross the binding. `operation_undo_checks` and `catalog_undos` hold ids, digests, codes and times (FORCE RLS, append-only, in the tenant-conformance registry); the snapshot it stores is store content under the existing snapshot rules.
- **Tenancy.** Another workspace's change, proposal, batch or store is `not_found`; the batch document is bound to the person and session; the picker's reads accept only the workspace's linked store.
- **One bad item refuses all.** The batch is one transaction; any member's refusal, a document that is stale, tampered or expired, or a phrase that does not match (or one given where none is needed) rolls back every decision. A replay of the same key replays every member; a second use of the document is refused.

## Deviations (reviewed with this PR)

1. Undo's 90 days are bounded by the before values' own clock: the draft's snapshot expires 90 days after Listings read the product, usually hours or days before the apply, so "available until" is the earlier of the two and the history shows that time.
2. ADR-0024 item 9 says a batch yields N approvals "with one `effectiveAt`": each member's cool-down starts at its own decision time, milliseconds apart in the one transaction. Each member records its own phrase digest (the ordinary path); the batch phrase is checked in code and not stored separately.
3. A batch is 2 to 10 proposals of one task (ADR-0024 allows 2 to 50 of one capability and store) until the decide path is measured on Neon.
4. A batch where only some members need the solo phrase, or where one that needs it carries a money cost, is not offered (the screen says to approve them one at a time).
5. The batch routes get a 15-second transaction (20-second request) instead of the review routes' 5 seconds: the document is rebuilt and every member decided in one transaction so that one refusal refuses them all. On this loaded Mac the 5-second budget timed the decision out (the lab's first run).

## Open questions

- Undo is proposed by the Listings installation (0032: the only proposer a catalog proposal may have), so under "Stop AI work" an undo can be prepared but not applied; the person changes it back in Shopify. Allowing a person's undo under that stop is an authority change for an ADR. Recommended: keep it as is for v1.
- Should a batch leave its own audit record (the document digest and the typed phrase's digest)? Today each member's decision is recorded with a command id derived from the batch key.
- The `linking` leg's time (for the lead): `lab:catalog-undo` takes about 10 minutes locally under load (CI unmeasured). If the leg passes its 20-minute cap, the lab moves to a leg with room, as `lab:catalog-apply` and `lab:plan-start` did.
