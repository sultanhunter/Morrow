## Goal

One durable job runner for every agent (`AgentRunWorkflow`, `agent-run@1`), in which the model calls Morrow's tools, each call is checked in code at call time and recorded, and a run can wait for a person's answer. Everything ships behind the agent-loop switch, committed off; nothing here can start a run on staging, and the switch cannot go on before the shared wallet's credit holds exist (ADR-0039).

## Scope

- **Records** (migration 0052, `morrow_work`): `agent_runs` (the pinned AgentRunManifest@1, caps, hold, counters; ids, digests, codes and amounts), `run_turns` (the only place loop content lives: each turn's input, the reply, the tool results; 90 days after the run ends; reasoning stripped at the end; each attempt's caps, `final` and `budget_ms`, fixed when it opens), `run_tool_calls` (RunToolCall@1, ids and digests; 365 days; born `counted` or not: a call over a cap or not offered is recorded uncounted and can only finish refused with that code), `agent_model_calls` (the broker's turn fence), `agent_run_questions` (at most one open per run; on `run_turns`' clock); the loop catalogue v2 (`loop_catalogue_versions`: its digest and stop conditions only, no money column; `loop_catalogue_entries`; committed `proposed`) and ToolCatalogue v2 (`tool_catalogue_entries`), drift-tested against the generated data. FORCE RLS on all eight; no DELETE or TRUNCATE.
- **Admission** (`packages/work/src/loop/admission.ts`, `cmd_agent_run_admit`): a person's routed run of one installed agent on one store. The switch first (`switch_off` before any statement), the manifest in code, then one definer that re-checks the task, store, stops, installation and release, takes only an `active` catalogue version, derives the caps (the version's turn limit, 16, never lowered by a money value) and the hold (the turn limit times the entry's worst-case turn: what every turn is booked against, not a cap), requires the operator test entitlement and a billing period (ADR-0027 item 6), closes the workspace's stale runs, and writes the lease, child task, envelope, Coordinator grant, hop and run. No money cap is counted or refused: no US$7 per run, no US$25 a day, no unrecovered-cost or look-for-work cap. The envelope and the hop carry exactly the offered tools' capability pairs and a scope of the run's store with exactly the tools' fields (`loop_tool_fields`, drift-tested against `authority.ts`'s `toolFieldScopes`). Its manifest CHECK proves the canonical text is exactly the run's columns. The answer (`cmd_agent_run_answer`) is the requester's only and opens the resume lease.
- **The runner** (`packages/work/src/loop/runner.ts`, steps in `steps.ts`): durable steps carry ids and codes only; every step re-enters the run lease. Before each turn, in code: the turn limit (the last turn it allows is final, `tool_choice: none`), the wall time (waits excluded) and the lease's time against the call's whole budget. `agent_turn_open` checks them again and fixes the attempt's caps (SQL derives `final` itself and compares); a replayed turn step takes the recorded caps, and every new send goes only while the wall time and the lease's time still cover its budget. An uncertain turn is booked at its worst case and sent again at most once, and only when SQL recorded it uncertain: a broker hold or the broker's own `uncertain` is settled against SQL first (a live fence is waited for, an expired one booked as uncertain, a committed reply read back); `agent_turn_open` also books an expired fence of attempt 1 before it opens attempt 2; a throttle resends with the next sequence; a lost reply is read back from SQL, never resent; a broker hold that left nothing in SQL is tried again at most twice (after 5 and 10 seconds) before the run ends `provider_error`. Each tool call: lease re-entry, replay of a recorded call (an unfinished uncounted call is refused again, never resumed), the full ToolInput validation, ids in scope, the authority decision in code, the handler. The wait step is safe to replay (`agent_run_resume_lease` says whether the wait committed). The end reason is derived in code. No money value starts, shortens or ends a run: cost past the hold is overflow, recorded and never dropped, and never a stop.
- **Authority per call** (`authority.ts`): `decideDelegation` over facts read again in the call's own step (`delegation_facts`, `run_billing_facts`): the requester's live grants with delegate, the envelope and the Coordinator's hop, the installation's grants and scope, the installation owner's authority, the policy revision, the store association and stops, the entitlement. Any one failing refuses the call (`authority_denied`, or `stopped`); missing facts refuse it.
- **Untrusted content** (ADR-0027 item 5): the request is the one text block; tool results enter only as tool results whose content names their `source`; a result carrying store, email or upload text taints the run; data blocks are wrapped by the adapter so they cannot close their wrapper. Tools, recipients and caps come from the manifest and SQL, never from text. The requester's answer enters the next turn as a text block.
- **The broker** (`apps/broker/src/agent-turn-gateway.ts`, `claude-turn-adapter.ts`): `RuntimeBroker.modelTurn` holds every call (`MODEL_UNAVAILABLE`) unless both the model-provider and the agent-loop switches are on, checks ModelCall@2 by hand, refuses `prefix_mismatch` before any request, fences the turn in SQL (`morrow_vault.agent_turn_begin`/`agent_turn_end`; the fence checks the lease, the run, the attempt as opened, the prefix and the turn limit, and no money), reads the selection, offered tools and system text from the run, counts, sends once, checks the reply against the run, and books the cost at the entry's rates (overflow never dropped). Strict tools, adaptive thinking, the effort, the system text cached for five minutes.
- **The orchestrator** (`apps/workers/src/orchestrator.ts`, `agent-run.ts`, `environment.ts`, `wrangler.jsonc`): `AgentRunWorkflow` beside `ListingsRunWorkflow` and main's runtime sweep; it throws `dispatch_disabled` before any step unless the agent-loop var is exactly `on` with `modelTurn` and the `AGENT_RUN` binding. The cron starts queued runs, wakes a waiting run's Workflow on its answer, and keeps agent-run leases out of the Listings starts. **Deploy order:** the cron reads `agent_run_pending_starts` on every tick, switch on or off, and skips the tick's starts when that read fails, so migration 0052 must be applied before the orchestrator carrying this code deploys (otherwise Listings starts pause until it is). The runner no longer imports the loop catalogue's rules, so `loop-catalogue.ts` and its generated data left the orchestrator's package allowlist.
- **The plan start's seam**: `PlanStepAdmitter` (`planStepAdmitterOff`); #58's plan start reads its loop switch from it and still reports every agent and specialist step `not_started` with `agent_loop_off`.
- **Tools composed in this build**: `inbox.ask` only. A tool without a handler is never offered.
- **The switch** (`docs/decisions/agent-loop-staging.json`, committed `off`): five prerequisites, each `null`: `catalogue_version_2_confirmed`, `gateway_reservations_stops`, `lab_agent_loop`, `shared_wallet` (ADR-0039's PR 3: loop turns and Listings' products hold and draw credits from the workspace's one wallet; it replaces `shared_daily_backstop`, in the record, `scripts/edge.mjs` and `scripts/generate-agent-platform.mjs`) and `store_content_cleanup`. `edge:check` refuses it on before every one is recorded with an evidence file, refuses it on without the model-provider switch (on in staging since #73), and refuses any Worker carrying `MORROW_AGENT_LOOP` while it is off.

## Security notes

- **Tenancy**: every new tenant table is FORCE RLS with the session and lease templates; the runtime login reaches records only through its step definers under the entered run lease (`agent_run_entered`, which also takes the run's lock); the broker's two definers reach only the run and call their own transaction-local settings name, as the broker's login; the cron's list and the lease lookup are ids only. The api role reads `agent_runs` column by column (no manifest text, lease, envelope, digest or tool list) and nothing else.
- **The intersection, in code**: every tool call is decided at call time over facts read in that step; a profile never changes a tool, capability or cap; no tool takes SQL, a URL, a store, a recipient, a credential or a model. The envelope and the hop are never wider than the offered tools (their pairs and fields); the per-turn and per-run tool-call caps hold across retried steps (an uncounted call is never resumed).
- **Unknown outcomes**: the fence commits before any request; an open fence past its budget is booked as uncertain at its worst case by the next step, finish, stale close or attempt 2's open; a late close is `already_closed`; the broker's answer is never SQL's verdict; an uncertain turn is resent at most once, only after SQL recorded it; a replayed turn step keeps the reply SQL recorded (and booked) under the caps the attempt was opened with.
- **Money (ADR-0039)**: no money cap in code or SQL, and nothing that stands in for one; permission and budget checks stay in code. Every counted turn is booked at the entry's rates (an uncertain one at its worst case); what passes the hold is overflow, recorded and never dropped. The budget invariant moves to the wallet: no loop run can run until ADR-0039's PR 3 holds credits under the wallet's row lock, because `edge:check` refuses the switch on before `shared_wallet` is recorded with its evidence; the owner's Anthropic monthly limit (US$100) is the outside backstop. Before the rate card a loop run runs only under the operator test entitlement.
- **Content**: conversation text lives only in `run_turns` and the questions, both on one clock (90 days after the run ends, ADR-0031 item 1; the questions' copy is open question 5); Workflow results, events, commands and logs carry ids and codes (checked by the unit tests and the lab).
- **Default deny**: the switch is committed off and refused on by `edge:check` until its prerequisites are recorded; catalogue v2 is committed `proposed`, so SQL admits nothing even with the switch on; production has no switch.

## Review findings and fixes (Tier 2: AI review, separate context, same model family)

First review (2026-10-24), five confirmed findings, each fixed with a test that fails without the fix:

- **F1 (medium), the broker's own `uncertain` taken as SQL's verdict.** Fix: `runTurn` settles a broker `uncertain` exactly as a hold and resends only when SQL recorded attempt 1 uncertain; `agent_turn_open` books an expired open call of attempt 1 before it opens attempt 2. Tests: `agent-loop.test.mjs` "the broker's own uncertain is settled against SQL"; the migration test's review block; lab U3 and U4.
- **F2 (medium), a lost `wait-q` result.** Fix: on a refused entry the wait step reads the run's status through `agent_run_resume_lease` (widened to the status and waiting runs). Tests: "a wait step whose result was lost after it committed goes on waiting"; the decoder; lab Q3.
- **F3 (medium), the per-run tool-call cap bypassed on a retried step.** Fix: `run_tool_calls.counted`, set at insert; an unfinished uncounted call is refused again with the same code, never resumed. Tests: "a call refused at the run's tool-call cap stays refused when its finish fails"; the migration test; lab C2.
- **F4 (low), admission's listings-only scope blocks the composed tool.** Fix: the envelope and the hop are scoped to the run's store and exactly the offered tools' fields (`loop_tool_fields`). Tests: the real kernel allows `inbox.ask` under the tools' field scope and refuses it under listings'; the drift test; lab A3.
- **F6 (low), two asks in one turn.** Fix: at most one open question per run (`question_limit`, the unique index `agent_run_questions_one_open`). Tests: "one open question at a time"; the migration test; lab Q1 and Q3.

Second review (2026-10-25), R1 and R3 confirmed, R2 not:

- **R1 (medium), the turn step not safe to replay.** Fix: `run_turns.final` and `run_turns.budget_ms`, set by `agent_turn_open` with the input and never changed (a different `final` is `invalid`, the same input with other caps a `conflict`); the runner checks the caps only for an attempt SQL has not opened; each new send checks the wall time and the lease's time against its budget; the broker refuses a call whose `final` or budget is not the attempt's (`turn_state`). With ADR-0039 the caps that could change between an open and its replay are only the time (no hold rule remains). Tests: "a turn step replayed after its close committed keeps its recorded reply and sends nothing" (a tool_use turn, a final turn past its hold, the wall time and lease passed after the close, a whole run with turn 1's result lost, an attempt whose step failed before any send); `agent-loop-migrations.test.mjs` "review R1"; lab U2, U3 and U5.
- **R3 (low), questions outside `run_turns` with no clock.** Fix: `agent_run_questions.expires_at`, born 90 days after the ask, moved by `agent_run_end` with the run's turns to 90 days after the end, only ever later. Tests: "review R3"; lab Q5.
- **R2 (low), not confirmed: the authority decision and a handler's write in two lease transactions.** The only handler, `inbox.ask`, writes one question to the run's requester inside Morrow, and the real kernel refuses it today (open question 4). Kept as a rule for the next handler: an internal write decides and writes in one transaction, as listings' `proposeDrafts` does.
