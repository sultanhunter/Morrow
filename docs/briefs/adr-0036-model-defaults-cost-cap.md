- Status: brief, 2026-10-25. Branch `claude/adr-0036-model-defaults-cost-cap`, from `main` at `500e87dc`, merged with `main` at `85cac200` (#63, #65) and at `3c08dc35` (#69, #71, #67). - **The owner's answers (OD-2026-10-25-2):** the PR was held as a draft until the owner confirmed version 3's default member ceiling (review F1). On 2026-10-25 the owner confirmed the ceiling of 4 (Q1, Q-adr-0036-member-ceiling answered), kept the installed default for the daily look-for-work pass and raised its cap to US$1.12 per agent per day (Q2, ADR-0036 item 7), and kept the US$7 and US$25 backstops (Q3).

## Goal

Carry out the owner's decision: Morrow, the main agent, defaults to Opus 5.5 at high; every other agent kind (specialists, sub-agents and every installed agent, Listings included) defaults to Opus 5.5 at medium; the US$4.50 unrecovered-cost cap is removed for Listings and loop runs. Keep the per-run US$7 and per-day US$25 backstops (reconfirmed by the owner), holds and reservations, the three-failure breaker, the Anthropic monthly limit and every authority rule. Carry out the owner's three follow-up answers (OD-2026-10-25-2): the default member ceiling of 4, and a look-for-work cap of US$1.12 per agent per day, two worst-case turns at the installed default, in place of 1% of the monthly grant.

## Security notes

- Budget checks stay in code: the US$7 run limit, the US$25 daily cap, credit and vendor reservations, the breaker, `cost_overflow` and `reservation_exhausted` are untouched; only the period cap goes. AI cost that earns no credit is bounded by US$25 a workspace a day and the Anthropic Console monthly limit.
- A profile is never authority: no tool, capability, grant or scope changes with a default.
- The look-for-work cap is checked in code, outside prompts: `lookForWorkHold` admits one pass a day per agent, held within US$1.12 (two worst-case turns at Opus 5.5 medium); a second pass the same day is `trigger_used` and a profile whose two worst-case turns exceed the cap is `cap_below_turn`. Verify refuses a loop version whose cap is missing, above the run backstop or below two worst-case turns of its installed default. The pass stays read-only (ADR-0024 item 6) and counts toward the workspace's US$25 daily backstop.
- The member ceiling follows the default (4 credits a product, the default's own credits; owner-confirmed, OD-2026-10-25-2); holders of `governance.model_default.revise` (`human_only`) still change it, and a workspace's stored ceiling and default are kept.
- Applied migrations are untouched; 0051 replaces 0033's helper only from its pinned body and lifts the append-only trigger for one status change inside its transaction.
- Deploy 0051 and the Workers together: between the two, Listings admission is refused `catalogue_inactive`, never admitted at the wrong version.
