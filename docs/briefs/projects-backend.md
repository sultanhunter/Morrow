## Goal

The records and rules behind the project flow, with no screen and every switch off: a project is one goal record with 1 to 3 measured goals; values and health come from code, per viewer; Everyday work areas and Quick tasks come from a person's tap on a code-built card; Morrow's plan cards create nothing until the person who asked taps Start, which creates every piece in one transaction under that person's live permissions.

## Security notes

- **Server-derived only:** start values (`startRef`), read tags, data classes, clocks, taint, restricted sources, the actor and the workspace. No body names a tenant, workspace, actor, grant, role or start value; a stored criterion with a non-null `startRef` fails the CHECK.
- **People only:** `workspace.goal.revise` and `workspace.goal.measure.record` are `human_only`; no tool writes a goal record, value, target or date. `plan.draft` grants nothing and creates no record but the card.
- **The plan start:** issued only for a digest the server holds, to the asker (or the person who opened a code-built card), on a standalone-workspace session; 5 minutes, single use, its nonce stored as a digest. The start re-runs the human kernel for every piece under live grants and the definer rechecks authority under lock; any refusal rolls everything back. It never admits a run. A plan change revises the project's goal record as the revise route does: its verb rule (own `execute`, another member's `administer`), goal view (404 without it, or when every goal is hidden from the person), and a goal-changing card refused (403) when the record holds a goal the person may not see. Morrow's card never sees a customer-class goal, so the start keeps each, unchanged, after the card's goals (a kept key the card also names, or a fourth measured goal, is 409), and `cmd_plan_start` refuses under lock any plan change that drops or alters one.
- **Before the plan-drafts switch goes on:** a record made from a tainted card is bound to that taint only through its consumed start document (purged after 24 hours) and its command record; ADR-0034 item 3 needs a durable per-record taint that runs and screens read, which lands with T6's message tables (the copied card) and T4's run context. Only Morrow's cards can be tainted, and they stay refused while the switch is off.
- **Quick tasks and areas:** only the Owner (ADR-0024; the solo-owner facts) gets and confirms a Quick tasks document, checked by the Worker and again by both definers under the organization interlock (`owner_only`). No plan change aims at Quick tasks (the Worker and `cmd_plan_start` refuse it, as `cmd_goal_revise` does).
- **Values and results:** the progress read takes only the rows the judgement uses (per group of key, product, assistant, read tags and class: the oldest, the latest and the latest on the oldest day), so it stays bounded; a hand check counts only in the measure's current assistant on a current product row. Finish's results record holds keys and health codes only: no measured number and no customer-class goal (it is permanent, untagged and readable by every member).
- **Customer data:** goal reads (`GET /goals`, `GET /goals/:goalId`, progress, revise and checks) leave a customer-class goal out unless the viewer may view `support.case.read` on the workspace's stores; a record left with no goal reads as absent (404, left out of a list), and a revise of a record with a hidden goal is refused (403), as is a goal-changing plan change. A read tag is viewable when the kernel allows `view` on its capability and store resource for the fields one of the viewer's own view grants on that capability names (a read tag names no fields of its own; with no such grant nothing is viewable). A value from sources tagged `support.case.read` is customer data: `goal_measure_values` refuses it by CHECK, and code leaves it out of every view but a permitted viewer's own read; it will live only in `goal_customer_values` (with Support's deletion path), expire with its cases, and never reach a goal record, a `project_update` or the Coordinator's context.
