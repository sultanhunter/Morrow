## Goal

One schedule contract for mandate routines, reports and policy triggers, in the shop's real IANA zone, that fires each slot exactly once through daylight-saving changes, never bursts after downtime, and never moves a schedule when the shop's zone changes until a person taps.

## Scope

- **Schedule@1** (v1.1 core, replacing v1's never-stored cron `Schedule`, declared in `changes.json`): `daily` (one to four local times, ascending, at least 15 minutes apart also across midnight), `weekly` (days in week order, one time), `monthly` (day 1 to 28 or `last`, one time), `every` (15, 30 or 60 minutes, or 2, 3, 4, 6 or 12 hours, as local times from midnight); every time on a 5-minute mark; the schedule's own IANA zone; `missedRun` `skip` or `run_latest_once`; optional first and last local dates.
- **The closed zone list** (`packages/contracts/src/time-zones.ts`, SQL twin `iana_time_zones()`): the 449 IANA zones that PostgreSQL 17 (tzdata 2026c) and Intl both know; 18 Intl spellings PostgreSQL lacks (`Asia/Calcutta`, `Europe/Kiev`, ...) map onto it. It replaces the four Canadian zones of Settings › Notifications, where the Owner may now override Shopify's zone with any zone of the list or follow Shopify's again (fixture-backed until the settings routes exist).
- **The slot arithmetic**, authoritative in SQL (`schedule_resolve_local`, `schedule_next_slot`, `schedule_fire_plan`) with a TypeScript twin (`packages/work/src/schedules/slots.ts`) for previews. Daylight saving: a local time that does not exist fires at the next valid minute, the moment the clocks change; a repeated local time fires once, at its first occurrence; local times that land on one instant fire once. Catch-up: every slot from the schedule's next slot to now is due; `skip` fires the latest only inside a 5-minute on-time window, `run_latest_once` fires the latest once inside the 24-hour replay horizon (ADR-0043 Values), and every other due slot is dropped; the next slot then moves past now.
- **The shop's zone from Shopify**: the broker's ShopIdentity document reads `ianaTimezone` at the install and every re-authorisation (a reconnect), and the daily health read's document reads it beside the granted scopes; each records it through `morrow_vault.shopify_zone_record` in the same vault transaction (the tenant and store from the installation itself). A zone off the list records nothing and never fails the install or the check.
- **A zone change moves nothing**: `store_time_zones` keeps Shopify's zone and the Owner's override (`store_time_zone_set`, Owner only); when the effective zone changes, `store_zone_apply` raises or revises one open `schedule_zone_notices` row per store ("Your shop's time zone changed to Asia/Kolkata. Move 2 schedules?") for the Owner and each listed schedule's owner, and clears it when nothing is left to move. `schedule_zone_move` (a recipient who is the Owner or the schedule's owner, at the revision they saw) moves one schedule; its slot is computed again in the new zone, and `schedule_revisions` records who moved it. The item's Inbox kind in `GET /work/inbox` and the tap's route come with the routine screens (build order PR 21 and plan row SD15), the first writers of schedules, since no schedule exists before them and an item nobody can act on would be a dead end; `schedule_zone_notices_open()` is the seam (as `connection_notices_open()` was for Reconnect). Until then the default-off Inbox preview (`samples.inbox`, fixtures only) shows the item in ADR-0043 item 8's words: "Your shop’s time zone changed to Asia/Kolkata", "Move 3 schedules? Each stays where it is until you move it."
- **Firing**: `fire_due_schedules(schedule id)` (ids only, `morrow_runtime_executor` only; the tenant comes from the schedule it names) writes one pending `mandate_triggers` row (MandateTrigger@1, kind `schedule`) per fired slot, keyed `sched:<schedule id>:<slot UTC>` and unique per tenant, due at its slot and expiring 24 hours later; at most 25 schedules a call. `schedule_tenants_due()` lists one due schedule id per tenant. The orchestrator's one-minute cron calls both under explicit time budgets (25 s a run, 5 s a transaction, 4 passes a tenant) behind the **`schedules-v2` switch**, committed off (`docs/decisions/schedules-v2-staging.json`, `MORROW_SCHEDULES_V2` on the staging orchestrator only); `edge:check` refuses an on record before build order PR 21's `admit_mandate_run` exists, and any mixed state.

## Security notes

- Tenancy: five new tables, each ENABLE and FORCE RLS with composite tenant keys and no PUBLIC grant; the command role holds SELECT and INSERT and column-level UPDATE only; nothing deletes (append-only triggers). The runtime and vault contexts are policies keyed to `morrow.schedule_*` settings and to the login (`session_user`); only this file's definers write those settings, each overwrites all four on entry and clears them before returning, and no other definer reads these tables (a later one that does must overwrite them too). Neither executor login holds a table privilege. `fire_due_schedules` takes a schedule id, never a tenant, time, slot or zone; a forged setting in another session reads nothing (lab M7).
- Authority: nothing here grants anything or has an external effect. Trigger rows are inert until PR 21's `admit_mandate_run`, which re-runs every admission check. A person's override and move recheck the session's authority under the organization's lock (`work_lock_and_recheck`), and the identity facts (the Owner, the schedule's owner, the recipients) are read live.
- Stops: the stop check at the writer is plan row SD14's `effect_stops_clear`, which does not exist yet. Until then a slot that falls due during a stop is written and admission (PR 21) must drop it; no burst follows a stop, since a slot older than the window never fires.
- Unknown outcomes: a lost acknowledgement of a firing pass is retried by the next minute's pass, which finds the slot advanced or the key written (one row per key, unique); nothing is assumed.
- Data: ids, closed-list zone names, local times, codes, counts and times only; no customer data, store text or secret. The zone is not customer data.
- Default off: firing waits for the switch; reading and recording the zone, the override, the Inbox item and the move work whatever the switch, and none of them has an effect outside Morrow.
