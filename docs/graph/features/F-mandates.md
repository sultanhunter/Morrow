An installed agent works on its own only under a mandate the Owner issues and renews every 90 days
and can revoke at once; revoking voids pending approvals. In a team the Owner installs and issues
with no second reviewer while no other member holds the mandate's store grants (ADR-0024 owner
question 1). V1-PR21.
