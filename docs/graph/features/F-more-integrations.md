Each provider beyond Shopify, Anthropic, E2B and Resend stays on the post-launch roadmap: the Meta,
Klaviyo, Gorgias, Google Search Console and storefront theme adapters (P17.T02a to P17.T02d and
P17.T02f), the OpenAI Images adapter (P17.T02e, post-launch under ADR-0022 item 10) and the
cross-functional scenario (P17.T05). P17.T01's operation contracts for these providers wait with
them; its support-reply slice ships with the Resend adapter (P17.T02g, F-support-email), as
ADR-0030 unblocks it.
