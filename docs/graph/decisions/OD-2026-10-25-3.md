Two messages in chat on 2026-10-25, quoted in the order sent. The first came during the lead's
review of the agent harness against Anthropic's article on folding subagents into one agent loop.
The owner then asked, "so what do you suggest subagents or main agent with skills and tool calls?",
and the lead recommended a main agent with scoped subagents: Morrow is the main loop (conversation,
questions, planning, project records, shop knowledge) and does not touch store data or draft store
changes itself; every teammate is a real subagent with its own loop, tool subset, permissions and
context, using skills and tools inside and never spawning subagents; Morrow calls a teammate like a
tool and gets a short structured result back, keeping the conversation, with a full hand-off only
when the teammate must talk to the person directly; small evals on the owner's real tasks measure
where a split earns its keep. The second message answers that recommendation.

Effect:
- Morrow is the main agent and keeps the conversation. Every other agent is a scoped subagent (a
  teammate), shown on the Team page as one list under Morrow.
- The separate, weaker, run-scoped specialist kind is dropped. There is one agent kind with two
  origins: agents built by Morrow's team, installed in one click, and agents Morrow creates on the
  go. A created agent may hold the permissions its job needs, and only after a person approves
  them on the exact card; it stays on the team until a person removes it.
- The lead told the owner, in reply, that these guardrails would be written in: Morrow proposes
  and people approve, and no agent, Morrow included, approves grants; no one grants more than they
  hold, and grants that write to the store, read customer data, spend money or message customers
  need the Owner; created agents use only tools from the fixed catalogue (no new code), and their
  instructions are shown on the approval card, with a taint mark if drafted after reading outside
  text; only Morrow may propose agents, and agents cannot create agents; created agents stay until
  removed, and Morrow may suggest retiring idle ones; any later permission increase is a new
  approval.
- ADR-0037 records the decision and amends the specialist records it replaces (ADR-0023 decisions
  1 and 11, ADR-0024, ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0028, ADR-0029, ADR-0031 and ADR-0034). Agent titles
  and delegation still confer no authority: only a person's approval of exact grants does.
