questions, each with one recommendation. The lead reads "approve ADR-0042 as recommended" as
accepting all 20 recommendations, and with them the record as recommended: every question carried
exactly one recommendation, and the sheet named "approve ADR-0042 as recommended" as the form of

committed by their own change (plan row CS2), not by this record.

The rest of the message sets the scope:

- **COD Confirmation is an installable agent.** It is Morrow's first-party package
  `morrow.cod-confirmation`, which a person adds to their workspace (question 7 (a)); agent cards
  hold no contact capabilities.
- **It is not built now.** No package, setup guide, plan screens, live provider qualification, eval
  rounds or contact switches are built for it until the owner asks for the agent.
- **The foundation is built now, to production grade.** Plan rows CS3 to CS9, CS11 and CS12 of
  `docs/plan/connected-services-implementation.md` become shared foundation, built behind switches
  that stay off: contracts and fakes, service connections and sealed entry, inbound events and call
  routes, health and limits, order events, plans and the contact gateway, order tags and
  cancellation proposals, and the contact data's deletion path. CS10's live qualification, CS13,
  CS14's COD-specific screens, CS15's COD rounds and CS16's contact switches wait until the owner
  asks for the agent.
- **Agents that use live store data and scheduled actions.** The general foundation for installable
  agents that consume live store data (orders, customers) and act on a schedule is question 17's
  answer, "(a) later, by its own change". That change is ADR 0043 (store events, schedules and
  standing action policies), proposed on the branch `claude/store-data-foundation-records`.
  Customer data still enters only after its P09.T05 deletion path, a CLAUDE.md invariant this
  message does not change; OD-2026-10-26-10 puts that path first.

Effect: ADR-0042 is Accepted 2026-10-26, with each of its 20 questions decided as recommended:

1. Services only from Morrow's catalogue, with the merchant's own accounts (items 2 and 3).
2. Keys through sealed entry that the broker signs, from a sub-account; never shown again; no account passwords (items 4 and 5).
3. Each new service needs its own one-page entry record that the owner approves (item 2).
4. Plivo calls (India region) first, its entry record still coming to the owner under question 3; WhatsApp second, through the provider the shop uses, once the owner names it. Which calling service and which WhatsApp provider the shop uses stays open until the owner asks for the agent (items 2 and 17).
5. Scripted, keypress-only calls; no free-form AI, recording, spoken answers or item names in v1 (items 11 and 15).
6. Standing call plans, with an edited plan's old revision running until the new one is approved, and the four CLAUDE.md lines exactly as the sheet shows them; ADR-0035 items 4 and 5 change with them (item 10).
7. (a): only the first-party COD Confirmation package holds contact capabilities in v1 (item 21).
8. Level 2 Phone only, never stored or shown, contact logs treated as personal data; ADR-0035 items 3 and 7 change for it (item 15).
9. `write_orders` requested during setup, with three append-only tags and held-out orders untagged (item 19).
10. Cancellation always by a person, in batches of up to 50, with the customer email off by default (item 20).
11. (a): India's transactional window only until counsel answers; attestations at connect; other countries closed (item 13).
12. One setup approval for the teammate and its first plan, and "Call me now" to the Owner's own phone, proven by a spoken code (items 10, 21 and 26).
13. Contact logs 90 days, daily counts 365 days, an opt-out kept as a fingerprint after a customer's deletion request, pending counsel (item 24).
14. The safety values as written in ADR-0042's Values table (items 10 and 13).
15. A 10% blind holdout fixed until about 300 settled held-out orders, an RTO source the Owner picks, and a weekly report (item 25).
16. (a): attempts metered, not charged, until rate card v1 (item 23).
17. (a): a general order-event trigger for every agent later, by its own change (item 18).
18. The merchant is the sender and controller and Morrow its processor; a merchant agreement, contact terms and a notice kit before outside merchants (item 1).
19. A workspace "Stop customer contact" for any Owner or Admin, with no second approver (item 14).
20. A parallel lane behind switches, with test calls after G10 and real customers after G9, Shopify's phone grant and the owner's one OK in chat, never holding launch; with the scope above, only the foundation is built now (item 27).

How each is carried out is written in ADR-0042 and `docs/plan/connected-services-implementation.md`.
