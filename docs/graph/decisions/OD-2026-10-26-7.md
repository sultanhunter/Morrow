The owner's message in chat on 2026-10-26, quoted in full, answering the combined decision sheet
for ADR-0040 (messaging with agents) and ADR-0041 (harness foundations),
Q-adr-0040-0041-owner-questions, which put 35 questions, each with the lead's recommendation. The
lead reads "accept the recommended options" as accepting all 35 recommendations, and with them both
records as recommended: that is the sheet's own example of approval ("approve ADR-0040 and ADR-0041
as recommended"), and every question on it carried one recommendation.

The rest of the message is a question, not a decision: whether a person can create an agent that,
when a cash-on-delivery order arrives, calls the customer to confirm it, so that fewer such orders
are cancelled, with the calling service and its API access provided by the merchant. The lead
answers it in a separate record. Nothing here decides it, and neither ADR-0040 nor ADR-0041
changes because of it.

Effect: ADR-0040 and ADR-0041 are Accepted 2026-10-26, with each question decided as recommended:

1. Opus 5.5 only: Morrow at high, teammates at medium; low effort leaves every catalogue; the per-agent picker offers teammates Opus 5.5 at medium or high only (extra-high and max later, once tests show they help); a change applies from the agent's next job (ADR-0041 item 2).
2. A running job renews its 30-minute lease at every step after re-checking the requester's permissions, the agent, the rules and any stop, inside an outer limit of 24 hours of active work or 7 days that only the requester can extend; waiting jobs hold no lease; the wider residual is accepted (ADR-0041 item 7).
3. Safety check-ins replace the old limits: a job pauses and asks at 1,000 turns, 10,000 tool calls, 24 hours of work, 7 days since the requester's last act or 32 teammate runs, and when code sees looping, repeated errors or no progress; catalogue values tuned by tests and staging; only the requester (for a scheduled agent, its owner or the Owner) continues; anyone holding a stop can stop; a teammate hands back to Morrow (ADR-0041 item 18).
4. A cost check-in at 5 times the usual credits for that kind of job, or the credits worth about US$25 of model use until staging has measured what is usual (ADR-0041 item 18).
5. At most 12 model calls in flight per workspace, and new jobs pause when Morrow's measured spend reaches 80% of the owner's Anthropic monthly limit, with a fair share per workspace; running jobs carry on (ADR-0041 item 9).
6. Out of credits, a job pauses ("Paused: out of credits", with Add credits and Stop), holds nothing, resumes by itself oldest first when credits are added, and closes after 7 days paused keeping its place for Continue; this replaces the final-turn reserve of OD-2026-10-26-1 item 2; the per-agent credit limit pauses only that agent (ADR-0041 item 9).
7. Compaction, the independent check, notebook and summary upkeep and resends are ordinary paid steps that set aside their worst case and pay what they used; failed jobs stay free, refunded at settlement; upkeep counts toward the member whose activity caused it (ADR-0041 item 9).
8. Holds count the cached part at the cache-read rate; a cache miss's difference is charged when the balance covers it and otherwise absorbed by Morrow, never ending a job (ADR-0041 item 9).
9. The whole growing conversation is cached: 1 hour for conversations, background requests and waits on a person; 1 hour on the fixed part and 5 minutes after it for short teammate calls (ADR-0041 item 10).
10. Streaming into the broker (never to a browser), Anthropic's beta on-demand compaction and context editing, and cache diagnostics are allowed; Morrow's own summarising method is built first and stays, behind a switch back, and the betas switch on only after passing the same tests and after Anthropic's retention terms are recorded (ADR-0041 items 8 and 11).
11. New job content is kept 90 days after each window of work closes, a long conversation starting a new window at least every 30 days; private reasoning is deleted once no longer sent; deleting a message, page or person removes every copy at once; content-free records 365 days (ADR-0041 item 14).
12. One current, private Morrow conversation per person, remembered as one continuing memory that is compacted and rebuilt when access narrows, with background requests branching off it, a Recent list with Working now and Done, and Start fresh keeping earlier conversations (ADR-0041 item 5; ADR-0040 item 11).
13. Personal memory is the person's alone (no Owner, Admin or member read path), saved only when the person taps Save, used only in their private Morrow conversation unless they set an entry to "everywhere I ask", never by scheduled agents; customer and contact details, card and ID numbers and passwords refused; no sensitive-topic suggestions (ADR-0041 item 29).
14. Personal memory is kept until the person deletes it, with "Still true?" after 180 days unused; erased within 24 hours of membership ending, at workspace erasure, and for a removed store's entries; it survives deleting its conversation, whose delete dialog offers to delete it too; deleting a memory erases every version (ADR-0041 item 29).
15. Morrow gets its own notebook of lessons (written only in shared work), memory suggestions and knowledge-page proposals, and a small upkeep run tidies its notebook, posting nothing (ADR-0041 items 24 and 27).
16. A teammate reads a lesson written for one person while working for another only when the new requester may see everything it came from, never from a private conversation; the agent page shows "learned while working for <name>" (ADR-0041 item 27).
17. Whoever's message or lesson shaped a proposed store change cannot approve it, and the review card names them; when everyone else in a team is blocked, the Owner approves with the solo owner's typed phrase and wait (ADR-0041 item 32).
18. A separate, fresh, read-only reviewer checks important work before Morrow presents it, at the effort of the work, and can only block; on by default with a workspace setting to turn it off; not used on everyday chat (ADR-0041 item 19).
19. 32 teammate runs per job envelope stay; at 32 the job asks its requester, whose Continue opens a fresh envelope for 32 more (ADR-0041 item 21).
20. Memory and knowledge search start word-based plus the agent's judgement; the paraphrase test is built now from the owner's store data and an embedding-provider record drafted beside it; below 95% of the right items in the top 5, that record comes to the owner before the memory features switch on (ADR-0041 item 30).
21. Approved now, used only if staging needs it: the runtime's database work through a small location-hinted Cloudflare object near the database, holding no data and with no web address (ADR-0041 item 35).
22. The owner labels about 60 example results once a quarter to calibrate the model judges (ADR-0041 item 37).
23. Until P09.T05, messages to agents with customer details are refused with "Morrow can't keep customer details yet. Remove them and send again."; order numbers stay allowed (ADR-0041 item 31; ADR-0040 item 2).
24. Morrow works like messaging a team of agents, as ADR-0040 describes it (ADR-0040 as written).
25. Option (a): a reply to a working request, shown as "Replying to", goes into it at its next step; with ✕ it goes to Morrow as a new message; quick bursts count as one; code routes, and only the requester steers (ADR-0040 items 7 and 8).
26. At most 5 working requests per person, then a waiting line of up to 20 for up to 24 hours, and a 2-second burst pause, as catalogue values (ADR-0040 item 9).
27. Live updates from launch through one content-free live channel per signed-in person (a Durable Object), with the browser checking every 2 seconds when it is down (ADR-0040 item 15).
28. Phone and desktop notifications from launch (Needs you at once, Done after 2 minutes unseen, at most 6 an hour, none in quiet hours, nothing private in them), the key held only by the broker, main push services only, subscriptions deleted on sign-out; one batched email after 15 minutes once the email domain is set up (ADR-0040 item 16).
29. "Seen by Morrow" (or the teammate's name) on people's messages, no read marks between people, and "Thanks" and "Not helpful" on agents' messages as feedback only (ADR-0040 item 14).
30. Standing requests on those terms (confirmed once, run with the person's permissions at the time, posting only in their own Morrow conversation, read and suggest only, 90 days unless renewed, at most 10 per person, paused or revoked by them or the Owner); teammates' routines report where they were set up, with a pointer when something needs the person (ADR-0040 item 12).
31. The person who started a request (the agent's owner for a scheduled agent, the signer for a standing request) can always stop it; the Owner and Admins get a workspace stop whose lifting is a reviewed step (ADR-0040 item 10).
32. Option (a): store-changing work, or work a teammate should own, starts with one tap on Start and runs in a project, followed in the Morrow conversation (ADR-0040 item 11).
33. People who can see a project conversation see that a request started there, its state, teammates and times, never a hidden step or content; a private Morrow conversation shows none of this to others (ADR-0040 item 1).
34. In the Morrow conversation only, the person's own messages sit on the right on a light warm-grey surface, with no coloured bubble and no emoji, after the owner sees the conversation drawn both ways (ADR-0040 item 11).
35. Milestone findings on long requests: only after 15 minutes of work, at most one per stage and one per 30 minutes, never claiming approval, change or completion, always with a status line built by code (ADR-0040 item 6).

How each is carried out is written in ADR-0040, ADR-0041 and `docs/plan/harness-foundations-implementation.md`.
