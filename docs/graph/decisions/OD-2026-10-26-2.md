> recommended

The owner's answer in chat on 2026-10-26 to the lead's question about Q-adr-0039-run-endings, put in plain
words with the lead's proposal: which job endings count as "failed" and are free, and whether to
add a breaker that pauses an agent after three failed jobs in a row. The proposal: free when the
job ended because of an error on Morrow's side or the AI provider's; charged when the job still
gave an answer, or a person stopped it; the breaker, yes. The message asked the owner to reply
"recommended" to accept all three.

Effect:

1. **Free endings:** a loop run that ends because of an error on Morrow's side or the AI
   provider's draws nothing.
2. **Charged endings:** a loop run that still gives an answer, or that a person stops, draws what
   its turns metered.
3. **A breaker:** after three failed loop runs in a row, an agent's runs and triggers in that
   workspace pause until a person resumes them.

The full list of end reasons in ADR-0039 item 2(g) is the lead's reading of this answer.
