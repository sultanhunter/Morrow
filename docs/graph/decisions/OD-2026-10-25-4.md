The owner's answer in chat on 2026-10-25 to the lead's message that put ADR-0037's listed values,
each with its recommendation, and the choice in the connector record of PR #74. For ADR-0037 the
message listed: (1) one teammate call or one hand-off per Morrow request at launch; (2) a
teammate's answer as a short structured result, a summary of up to 2,000 characters plus typed data
(8 KiB); (3) at most 10 Morrow-created teammates per workspace, and a suggestion to retire one idle
for 30 days; (4) a created teammate's budget of 5% of the monthly credits per job and 20% per day,
plus US$7 per job and US$25 per day; (5) customer data stays with Support at launch; (6) adding a
created teammate is an access change (the Owner or an Admin; a solo Owner types the confirmation
phrase); (7) in a private Ask, a new teammate only through a plan card's step; (8) uploads work the
same for created teammates. For the connector record it offered the connector's scheduled jobs in a
Durable Object near the database (recommended) or longer time limits.

Effect, as the lead carried it out in reply:
1. **Money: credits are the only spend limit.** Every fixed money cap goes: US$7 per run, US$25
   per workspace per day, the daily look-for-work cap (US$1.12, set earlier the same day on
   PR #70), the created-teammate caps of 5% per run and 20% per day, and any other hardcoded money
   ceiling. Each job reserves and draws credits from the workspace balance as it works; when the
   balance cannot cover the next step, the work stops. The owner's Anthropic monthly limit
   (US$100, set in the owner's Anthropic account) stays as the outside backstop. Non-money safety
   limits stay: each job's stop conditions (a generous wall-time limit and a maximum number of
   turns or steps), so a stuck or looping job ends, and the owner can stop any job at once.
   Settings the Owner controls, such as the member model ceiling, stay settings, not hardcoded
   money caps. A money record of its own writes the rule down; it reverses the owner's earlier
   answers of the same day on PR #70 that set the US$1.12 cap and kept US$7 and US$25.
2. **Orchestration: truly multi-agent.** Morrow may orchestrate teammates in any way: many calls,
   in sequence or in parallel, the same teammate more than once, and hand-offs. Teammates still
   never spawn subagents (depth 1), and every call is still checked in code (ADR-0037 item 3).
3. **Results: structured, with no character cap.** Large outputs, such as tables and files, are
   stored as items referenced by id, which Morrow opens in full with a tool when it needs them, so
   nothing is cut and Morrow's context does not overflow (ADR-0037 item 3(a)).
4. **Items 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 as recommended.** Item 4 is replaced by credits only (effect 1).
5. **The connector record (PR #74), as recommended:** the connector's scheduled jobs run in a
   Durable Object near the database.
