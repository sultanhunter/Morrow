The owner's answer in chat on 2026-10-25 to the lead's message that listed the eight questions of
the agent architecture record (proposed on the branch `claude/adr-0037-agent-architecture`), each
with its recommendation (1: one teammate call or one hand-off per Morrow request at
launch; 2: a teammate's answer as a short structured result, a summary of up to 2,000 characters
plus typed data of up to 8 KiB; 3: at most 10 Morrow-created teammates per workspace, and a
suggestion to retire one idle for 30 days; 4: a created teammate's budget of 5% of monthly credits
per job and 20% per day, plus US$7 per job and US$25 per day; 5: customer data stays with Support
at launch; 6: adding a created teammate is an access change, by an Owner or Admin, with a typed
phrase for a solo owner; 7: in a private Ask, a new teammate only through a plan card step; 8:
uploads the same for created teammates), and ADR-0038 (the connector's cron in a Durable Object
near the database, recommended) against longer time limits.

Effect, as the lead stated it to the owner in reply:

- Money: credits are the only spend limit. Every fixed money cap is removed: US$7 per run, US$25
  per workspace per day, the look-for-work daily cap (US$1.12), the created-teammate 5% per run
  and 20% per day caps, and any other hardcoded money ceiling. Each job reserves and draws credits
  from the workspace balance as it works; when the balance cannot cover the next step, the work
  stops. The owner's Anthropic monthly limit (US$100, set in the owner's Anthropic account) stays
  as the outside backstop. Safety limits that are not money stay: a job's stop conditions (a
  generous wall-time limit and a maximum number of turns or steps), so a stuck or looping job
  ends, and the owner can stop any job at once. Settings the Owner configures (for example the
  member model ceiling) stay settings, not hardcoded money caps.
- Orchestration (question 1): truly multi-agent. Morrow may orchestrate teammates in any way: many
  calls, sequential or parallel, the same teammate more than once, and hand-offs. Teammates still
  never spawn subagents (depth 1).
- Results (question 2): structured, with no character cap. Large outputs (tables, files) are
  stored as items referenced by id, which Morrow opens in full with a tool when needed, so nothing
  is stripped and Morrow's context does not overflow.
- Questions 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 take their recommended answers. Question 4 is replaced by the
  credits-only rule above.
- ADR-0038: "use the recommended one" is taken as approval of the recommended option, the Durable
  Object that runs the connector's scheduled jobs beside the database. ADR-0038 is accepted as
  written.
