beside US$7 per job and US$25 per day; 5, customer data staying with Support at launch; 6, adding

teammates. The same message asked about the connector's scheduled jobs: a Durable Object near the

The lead replied with the decision as it is carried out, recorded here as the effect.

Effect:
- **Money: credits are the only spend limit.** Every fixed money cap goes: US$7 per run, US$25 per
  workspace per day, the look-for-work cap of US$1.12 per agent per day, the created-teammate caps
  of 5% of the monthly credits per run and 20% per day, and every other hardcoded money ceiling.
  Each job reserves and draws credits from the workspace balance as it works; when the balance
  cannot cover the next step, work stops. The owner's Anthropic monthly limit (US$100, set in the
  owner's Anthropic account) stays as the outside backstop. Non-money safety limits stay: a job's
  stop conditions (a generous wall-time limit and a maximum number of turns or steps) so that a
  stuck or looping job ends, and the owner can stop any job at once. Settings the Owner controls,
  such as the member model ceiling, stay settings. ADR-0039 records this part.
- **Orchestration: truly multi-agent.** Morrow may orchestrate teammates in any way: many calls,
  in sequence or in parallel, the same teammate more than once, and hand-offs. Teammates still
  never start subagents (depth 1).
- **Results: structured, with no character cap.** Large outputs (tables, files) are stored as
  items referenced by id, which Morrow opens in full with a tool when it needs them, so nothing is
  stripped and Morrow's context does not overflow.
- Items 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 as recommended. Item 4 is replaced by credits only.
- The connector's scheduled jobs: the recommended option, a Durable Object near the database.

The orchestration, results and items 3 and 5 to 8 belong to the main-agent record on pull request
72, and the scheduled jobs to the connector record on pull request 74; each carries its part.
