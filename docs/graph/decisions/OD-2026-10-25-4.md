Effect:
- **Money: credits are the only spend limit.** Every fixed money cap goes: US$7 per run, US$25 per
  workspace per day, the look-for-work daily cap (US$1.12), the created-teammate caps of 5% per
  run and 20% per day, and any other hardcoded money ceiling. Each job reserves and draws credits
  from the workspace balance as it works; when the balance cannot cover the next step, work stops.
  The owner's Anthropic monthly limit (US$100, set in the owner's Anthropic account) stays as the
  outside backstop. Non-money safety limits stay: a job's stop conditions (a generous wall-time
  limit and a maximum number of turns or steps), so a stuck or looping job ends; the owner can
  stop any job at once. Settings the Owner can configure (for example the member model ceiling)
  remain settings the Owner controls, not hardcoded money caps.
- **Orchestration: truly multi-agent.** Morrow may orchestrate teammates in any way: many calls,
  in sequence or in parallel, the same teammate more than once, and hand-offs. Teammates still
  never spawn subagents (depth 1).
- **Results: structured, with no character cap.** Large outputs (tables, files) are stored as
  items referenced by id, which Morrow opens in full with a tool when needed, so nothing is
  stripped and Morrow's context does not overflow.
- Items 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8 as recommended. Item 4 is replaced by credits only.
- The connector record: "use the recommended one" means the recommended option, the Durable Object
  that runs the connector's scheduled jobs.

Where each part is written down: the money rule in a money record of its own (pull request 75);
the orchestration, the results and items 3 and 5 to 8 in the main-agent record (pull request 72);
the scheduled jobs in the connector record (pull request 74).
