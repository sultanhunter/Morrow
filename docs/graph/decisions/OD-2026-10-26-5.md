> one more thing i am seeing we are using haiku for some parts? why is that? we don't want to cheap out on models as we want the best results for users as all the users are going to be paid users

The owner's message in chat on 2026-10-26. At the time, the per-agent picker (ADR-0036 item 1) and
Listings' per-product catalogue (ADR-0022 item 16) offered Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 beside Opus 5.5.

Effect:

1. **No Haiku, and no model cheaper than Opus 5.5, for paid users.** Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5
   leave the offer of every catalogue, Listings' per-product catalogue included. A published
   catalogue version never changes, so each catalogue drops them in a new version (ADR-0041 item 2;
   the amendment notes of 2026-10-26 in ADR-0022, ADR-0027 and ADR-0036).
2. **The defaults stand** (OD-2026-10-25-1): Morrow on Opus 5.5 at high, every other agent on
   Opus 5.5 at medium.

Not decided by this message, and put to the owner as ADR-0041 owner question 1
(Q-adr-0040-0041-owner-questions): the lead's reading that `low` effort goes too, and whether the
Owner may still raise a single teammate to high effort.
