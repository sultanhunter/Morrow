The owner's answers in chat on the evening of 2026-10-27, on two question cards, to open points
that OD-2026-10-27-1 and its pull request (94) left: when design partners start on the private
US$99 plan of ADR-0026's 2026-10-27 note, and how many credits that plan includes. Each answer is
quoted above in full, exactly as written; the questions follow as they were asked.

How it came about. Pull request 94's open points 3 and 4 said that the private plan's grant at the
default plan's price per credit was the lead's reading of ADR-0026 item 5, not the owner's answer,
and that a design partner cannot install or be billed before the public launch unless the owner
decides otherwise (ADR-0035 items 2 and 3). Earlier in the same exchange the owner wrote "yes ok
from my side and can you explain these 3 points better?"; the lead did not treat "yes ok" as a
decision, because the owner asked for the explanation. Before the first card, the lead explained
three points:

1. The private US$99 plan cannot start before the public launch. It exists only through Shopify
   App Pricing, which ADR-0035 item 3 holds until the first outside merchant, and ADR-0035 item 2
   lets no other store link before the public launch. Using it earlier would need a new ADR
   changing ADR-0035, the customer-data deletion path first, and Shopify's confirmation on installs
   before App Review.
2. The private plan's grant was the lead's reading: the same price per credit (about two-thirds of
   the US$149 plan's grant), so no other merchant's rates change. The alternative, the full grant
   at US$99, would, under ADR-0026 item 5's margin rule, raise every merchant's credit rates by
   about 50%.
3. The owner-only steps: create the private plan in the Partner Dashboard (the owner's own login),
   name each design partner, and buy one month of StoreLeads Pro (US$250) when outreach starts.

## The lead's reading

This is the lead's reading of the answers, open to the owner's correction. Only the grant is the
owner's choice of an offered option; the rest reads the owner's free text.

- **Design partners wait for results on the owner's own store.** No design partner and no other
  store before Morrow gives results on the owner's own store. The private US$99 plan is offered
  only after that, and only with App Pricing (track T13). "Pilot before launch" was not chosen.
  ADR-0035 items 2 and 3 stand unchanged: the owner's store is the only real store until the
  public launch, and App Pricing waits for the first outside merchant.
- **The private plan's grant keeps the default plan's price per credit.** It is now the owner's
  choice ("Same price per credit (Recommended)"), no longer only the lead's reading of ADR-0026
  item 5. The formula of ADR-0026's 2026-10-27 note stands: the default plan's grant times 99
  divided by the default plan's monthly price, rounded down to a whole credit.
- **The priority: build the best harness.** The harness foundation
  (`docs/plan/harness-foundations-implementation.md`) and the store-data foundation
  (`docs/plan/store-data-foundation-implementation.md`) are built to the full standard; nothing
  dropshipping-specific is built now. The owner will iterate on the owner's own store. No supplier
  connector, no supplier-order placement and no new money path is decided; any of those later
  needs its own record, since provider selection and money are material under ADR-0002.
- **Going to market waits for results.** The go-to-market decisions of OD-2026-10-27-1 stay
  recorded, but outreach to Indian brands and to design partners waits for results on the owner's
  store. The 2027-01-31 COD decision date stands as recorded unless the owner moves it.
