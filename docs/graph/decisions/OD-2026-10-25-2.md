> Q1: "With Listings on Opus 5.5 medium, each product it drafts costs 4 credits instead of 1 (your 300 test credits = about 75 products). Who in a shop may run it at that default?" Answer: "Everyone, up to 4 (Recommended)"

> Q2: "Each agent's daily 'look for useful work' pass (read-only, proposes nothing) has a cost cap of about 1% of the plan price. One Opus 5.5 medium pass costs more than that cap, so these passes would never run. What should they use?" Answer: "Opus medium, raise the cap" (the option said: keep Opus 5.5 medium even for the daily check and raise its cap to fit, about US$1.12 per agent per day worst case)

> Q3: "With the unbilled-AI-cost cap removed, the remaining safety limits are US$7 per run and US$25 per workspace per day, plus your Anthropic monthly limit. Keep those two?" Answer: "Keep US$7 / US$25 (Recommended)"

The owner's answers in chat on 2026-10-25 to three multiple-choice questions the lead put after
OD-2026-10-25-1. Each quote is the question as put and the option the owner chose, verbatim; Q2's
also gives what the chosen option said. ADR-0036 quotes them in its Approval section.

Effect:
1. Catalogue version 3's default member ceiling of 4 credits a product is confirmed (ADR-0036
   item 2), which answers Q-adr-0036-member-ceiling. A Member without
   `governance.model_default.revise` may run Listings at the default, Opus 5.5 at medium, and any
   entry of at most 4 credits a product. It replaces the ceiling of 1 the owner confirmed on
   2026-10-19.
2. The daily look-for-work pass keeps the installed default, Opus 5.5 at medium. Its cap becomes
   US$1.12 of worst-case vendor cost per agent per day, two worst-case turns at that default, in
   place of ADR-0026 item 4's 1% of the monthly grant per pass (ADR-0036 item 7). One pass per
   agent per day stays (ADR-0024 item 6); a costlier profile, such as Opus 5.5 at high (two turns
   US$1.28), is not admitted for the pass.
3. The per-run US$7 and per-workspace US$25 a day backstops stay, with the owner's Anthropic
   monthly limit (ADR-0026 item 4, owner question 6).
