> recommended

The owner's answer in chat on 2026-10-27, in full, to the lead's list of six go-to-market
decisions.

How it came about. On 2026-10-27 the lead published a research report to the owner, "Morrow: the
platform direction, five example agents, and going to market in Delhi NCR and Hyderabad" (private:
https://claude.ai/artifact/SwZovVyTmdKNF5zHP1YYcY). Its sections 3 (the five example agents), 4
(which agents to lead with for Indian D2C brands, and whether "COD later" changes), 5.4 (pricing in
rupees), 6.3 (the outreach sequence), 6.4 (the legal limits) and 7.1 (the owner decisions this
direction needs) hold the facts and sources below. The lead then sent the owner the six decisions
below in chat, preceded by the line: You can reply "recommended" to accept all of these:. The owner
answered "recommended". The lead reads it as accepting these six decisions as written, and nothing
else.

The six decisions, as proposed, which "recommended" accepts:

1. **India launch agents:** lead with Listings, Inventory and the weekly RTO report. Offer COD
   confirmation as a waitlist for the first add-on. *Recommended.*
2. **COD timing:** move it from "later" to "first India add-on after launch", and decide on 31
   January based on demand from your meetings. *Recommended.*
3. **COD channel:** WhatsApp first, on a rupee-billed account, instead of the Plivo voice calls
   ADR-0042 picked first. It's cheaper and safer under TRAI's new rules, and Plivo's own terms ask
   for explicit consent anyway. *Recommended.* It changes a provider choice, so I'd record it
   formally, and a lawyer still needs to answer the India questions before any outside merchant.
4. **India price:** US$149 is about ₹14,400 on a Shopify bill. Add any Shopify plan and the total
   crosses ₹15,000, so the merchant has to approve the bill by bank OTP every month.
   *Recommended:* ask US$149 by default, and let yourself offer a private US$99 plan (about ₹9,600)
   to named design partners when price or the OTP blocks a commitment.
5. **Spend:** about ₹1.5–4.1 lakh over 90 days. It starts with one month of StoreLeads Pro (US$250,
   about ₹23,800) to count and list the brands doing 1,000–10,000 orders a month. That purchase is
   yours to make; I won't buy anything.
6. **Audits:** make the storefront audits outside Morrow until launch, so no other store's data
   enters the live environment. *Recommended.*

  milestone and the agent AG-business-analyst are governed by this record. Listings and Inventory
  are already launch agents.
  The waitlist for COD Confirmation and the brands on it are sales records kept outside this
  repository. The lead suggests that the waitlist also note which WhatsApp provider each brand
  uses; decision 1 does not say so.
- **Decision 2, COD timing.** ADR-0042's Amendments (item 27): COD Confirmation stays an installable

- **Decision 3, WhatsApp first.** This amends OD-2026-10-26-9's answer to ADR-0042 owner question 4

  owner's approval with that record. Counsel's questions (item 13, G16) gain four: whether Plivo's

  and whether the draft CCPA guidelines on unsolicited communications reach a merchant's service
