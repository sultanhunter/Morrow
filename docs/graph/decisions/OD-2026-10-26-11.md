Effect: ADR-0043 and ADR-0044 are Accepted 2026-10-26, and the note on ADR-0031 item 1 is
decided, with each question decided as recommended:

1. Store events may start any installed agent's work: a subscription to new or changed orders, products or stock either starts the agent or runs an approved action in code, and Morrow acts only on ids in its own rows, never on the text Shopify sends (ADR-0043 items 3 to 6).
2. Events that start an agent are grouped over 5 minutes; an agent starts at most 6 runs an hour from events, deadlines and monitors together; a row names up to 500 records; missed events are replayed for 24 hours; all catalogue values (ADR-0043 item 5 and Values).
3. Deadlines and monitors are checked by code, which posts the alert itself, at most once per window; the AI runs only to explain or draft, at most once per window (ADR-0043 items 10 and 11).
4. Agents use only the Shopify topics ADR-0025 item 6 already approves; others come one agent at a time, each by a dated amendment with its own data trimming (ADR-0043 item 3).
5. One approval of an exact standing policy covers each small, reversible action inside it, admitted by code and applied, read back and recorded by the gateway: Morrow's own order tags or tags the Owner picks, removing a tag the policy added, and a note in Morrow's own field on an order; ADR 0042's call plans become the first standing policy, as its one named exception under their own approval; anything new joins only by a dated amendment; and CLAUDE.md's wording changes to match (ADR-0043 items 12 to 17).
6. Cancelling and refunding orders, prices, discounts, stock quantities, fulfilment, changes to customers, supplier mail and anything that cannot be undone need the owner's approval each time, alone or in a batch; no answer, deadline or monitor cancels an order by itself (ADR-0043 item 16).
7. A plan bundle covers 2 to 50 pending changes of up to 5 kinds on one store, with one phrase listing each kind's count, one cool-down and the strictest rule among them; one bad change refuses the whole bundle (ADR-0043 item 18).
8. Pause rule (the Owner, Admins and the agent's owner) and Revoke rule (the Owner and the agent's owner) need no second person, never wait on credits, and agents may only suggest them; turning a rule back on is reviewed like approving it (ADR-0043 item 19).
9. One stop check in code for every automated path; anything that falls due while stopped is skipped, not saved up; resuming covers only what happens afterwards unless the person opts earlier ones in; "Stop customer contact" covers every contact capability; the workspace stop lands before any send or order-write switch goes on (ADR-0043 items 20 and 21).
10. Option (a): work that runs without AI is counted but not charged until rate card v1 prices it, never blocked by credits when time-critical; AI runs hold credits as today; agents waiting on credits are combined when credits return; discretionary bulk work reserves all its credits at approval (ADR-0043 item 22).
11. Installed agents may ask questions and post, only in their own Everyday work area and the threads of tasks given to them; a teammate that Morrow calls still never talks to a person (ADR-0044 items 1 to 3).
12. The two abilities are two rows on the install screen or agent card, on by default when the agent declares them and covered by the same approval; the agent's owner or the Owner can switch them off at once; switching them back on is reviewed (ADR-0044 item 5).
13. Option (a): the person who added the agent, or the Owner, answers for work it does on its own, and the person who handed it a task answers for that task; whoever answers counts as an author of any change the answer shapes (ADR-0044 items 4 and 8).
14. One open question per job and three per agent, each expiring after 7 days with reminders at 24 and 72 hours; at most 3 findings per job and 20 a day per agent; no mentions, Morrow's code sends the notifications; all catalogue values (ADR-0044 item 6).
15. Counsel's question on India's one-year log rule stays open.
16. Option (a): no paging service yet; operator alerts go to a named person through the paths Morrow already has, and the choice is made again before the first outside merchant or when alert volume shows a need (plan row SD22).
17. Option (a): Morrow moves to Neon's paid plan before store events run around the clock. The owner bought it on 2026-10-26, before the order-events switch, which stays off.

**Review fixes after the answer.** An AI review of the records (separate context, same model
family) ran on the text the owner answered. Its fixes were made the same day, after the answer,
and a second review of 2026-10-27 corrected them (below). Each narrows or makes exact what the
owner approved, and none widens it:

- Question 5: `contact.attempt.send`, which ADR 0042 makes irreversible, is not on the reversible allowlist; the call plan is the one named exception, under ADR 0042's own approval and checks (`standing_policy: contact_plan`), and a test keeps every other eligible capability reversible and outside the communication, spend and billable classes. Admission takes only the id of an occurrence row that code wrote; an action is unique across revisions; the gateway re-checks the policy's predicates on its live read. A schedule acts only on subjects that a closed subject query in the policy's digest selects.
- Question 9: lifting any stop resumes no rule by itself, as ADR 0042 item 14 says of call plans; opting earlier subjects in writes new occurrence rows from SQL, and a skipped row stays ended.
- Question 10: agents waiting on credits are still combined into one run each, as answered; each waiting row is re-checked as it merges (its trigger still active, its mandate active, no stop), and one that fails ends instead of riding on another's run. A job's own follow-through for later (a `once` schedule) resumes on its own, as a paused job does.
- Question 15: order ids in trigger rows, occurrence rows and rule-action logs are blanked 90 days after the order's date, not kept 365 days; a contact's per-order outcome lives only in ADR 0042's contact tables.
- Question 13: the question tool takes no respondent field; code computes the respondent from the run's kind. A question offers at most 5 choices, the existing tool contract's bound.
- Question 12: switching the abilities off is its own `human_only` kill switch, `agents.installation.collaboration.disable`.
- The routine schedule items: code-written `once` schedules are bounded (15 minutes ahead, counted in the hourly rate, a successor episode that inherits taint and read tags, no chains) and recorded as an amendment of ADR-0041 items 7 and 20 rather than as routine.
- Smaller fixes: the reconciler runs from the orchestrator's cron, leaving ADR-0038 item 2 unchanged; alert titles are rendered from ids; notifications stay content-free; a tag the Owner picks carries a warning, and a removal never strips a tag the merchant set; every rule control records a reason, an audit record and a notice (ADR-0005 item 1).

**Second review** (2026-10-27). A second AI review (separate context, same model family) found
that the first round's question 10 fix, which combined waiting runs per trigger rather than per
agent, widened the answer: an agent with two waiting triggers would have resumed as two runs. It

Its other fixes make exact what was answered:

- Question 9: "Stop customer contact" stops every contact capability that calls or messages
  someone and every action of a call plan, its order tags included, as ADR 0042 approved, and
  never blocks the contact controls or reading results; a tag rule that is not a call plan keeps
  running. The one stop check works out the workspace and connections itself, from the agent and
  the store, and treats an unknown or mismatched id as stopped.
- Question 5: removing a tag is its own rule, paired with the rule that adds the tag and approved
  with it on one card; it removes only a tag its pair added, with no removal since.
- Question 13: a person asking a question still names who answers, as today; only an agent's
  questions get their answerer from code.
- Not asked (the schedule items): a job's follow-through for later ends with the job when a stop,
  a revoke, a kill switch, an authority failure or the mandate's end ends it, and runs only under
  the mandate it was written under; one over the hourly rate waits for the next slot. How late an
  event may arrive and still start work is a catalogue value: 24 hours by default, from 5 minutes
  to 24 hours.
