> recommended

The owner's answer in chat on 2026-10-26, in full, to the lead's proposal to simplify the limits
that the owner had accepted earlier that day with the decision sheet for ADR-0040 and ADR-0041
(OD-2026-10-26-7), so that credits are the only budget.

How it came about. Earlier on 2026-10-26 the app delivered again an owner message of 2026-10-25
that is already recorded as OD-2026-10-25-4 ("for budget why we limiting anything? it should be
very simple credit based system ..."). The lead answered it with this proposal. The owner then said
they had not sent that message again and asked how it got sent. This record does not establish how
the app came to deliver it again. The re-delivered message decides nothing here: it is not a new
statement by the owner, and OD-2026-10-25-4 stays its only record. The lead then put the proposal
to the owner again, and the owner answered "recommended". That answer, to that proposal, is the
decision.

The proposal, which "recommended" accepts in full:

Removed:

1. **The cost check-in** (OD-2026-10-26-7, question 4; ADR-0041 item 18): a job no longer checks in
   when it has used 5 times the usual credits for its kind of job, or, until staging has measured
   what is usual, the credits worth about US$25 of model use.
2. **The 80% pause** (question 5; ADR-0041 item 9): new jobs no longer pause when Morrow's measured
   Anthropic spend this month reaches 80% of the owner's Anthropic monthly limit, and the fair share
   per workspace that came with the pause goes too. In its place, code raises an alert to the owner,
   as Morrow's operator, and to no one else. A job parks on the provider's limit only when Anthropic
   actually refuses a call (`spend_limit`, ADR-0041 item 8).
3. **The per-person caps** (ADR-0040): at most 5 requests working at once and 20 waiting per person
   (question 26, item 9), and at most 10 standing requests per person (question 30, item 12). Work
   queues only when the system is busy.

Kept:

4. **12 model calls in flight per workspace**, as traffic control: jobs wait for a free slot and are
   never refused (question 5; ADR-0041 item 9).
5. **The stuck-agent check-ins and detectors that pause and ask:** 1,000 steps, 10,000 tool calls,
   24 hours of active work, 7 days since the requester's last act, repeated errors, and "cost without
   progress", the one detector measured in credits, which trips on credits spent since the run's last
   progress against the run's own median, never against a total (question 3; ADR-0041 item 18). They
   protect the shop's credits from loops, and they never end work or cap spend.
6. **The platform ceiling on buying extra credits:** twice the plan price per cycle (OD-2026-10-26-1;
   ADR-0039, "Kept, and why"). It bounds what Morrow may put on a merchant's Shopify bill in one
   cycle, not what work spends.
7. **The caps on contacting customers** (carrier and legal rules) that ADR 0042 (connected services
   and customer contact, the record that answers the owner's question about an agent calling
   customers to confirm cash-on-delivery orders) sets: at most 3 contacts per number in any 24 hours
   and 6 in any 30 days, and a daily limit per call plan in COD orders that the Owner sets. ADR 0042
   calls them safety bounds, not money.
8. **The optional per-agent credit limit**, off unless the Owner sets one (OD-2026-10-26-1; ADR-0039
   item 7).
9. **32 children per job envelope**, then the requester's Continue: an authority check-in, not a
   budget (question 19; ADR-0041 item 21).

Effect: credits are the only budget. OD-2026-10-26-7's answers to questions 4, 5, 26 and 30 change as
above; its other answers stand, question 26's 2-second burst pause among them, since it gathers a
quick burst of messages into one request and limits nothing. With the waiting line gone, so do its
24 hours and the refusal `too_many_waiting`. With the 80% pause and its fair share per workspace
gone, only a workspace's own credits and its 12 calls in flight bound how much of the owner's
Anthropic monthly limit, which every workspace shares, it can use (the risk ADR-0039 names under
Consequences): one workspace's paid work can reach the limit, every workspace's jobs then park on
`spend_limit`, and the 80% alert is the owner's warning to raise the limit. Nothing else changes:
the three-failure breaker (OD-2026-10-26-2), the member credit ceiling and the Owner's other
settings, Listings' limits, and every authority and security rule.
