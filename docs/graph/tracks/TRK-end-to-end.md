A simulated week (W8 to W9), submission (W10), review and fixes (W11 to W16), launch. A moderated
test of the flow with five merchants on the prototype (ADR-0033 item 12), which also carries the
P02.A04 participant study: 4 of 5 find the weekly stock check under Stock, read a project's date as
when it ends, restate its "Done when", pick the right project, answer "Does anything need you?"
within 5 seconds, undo an approved change within 60 seconds and state a project's cost and balance
left; 5 of 5 find Stop within 10 seconds. The cost per Morrow request is measured.
